THE REVIEW

Why it scores well

Tenable lists 117K+ CVEs and 319K+ plugins, plus more than 450 pre-configured scan templates. Professional includes unlimited scanning, and price and missing SSO details pull the score down.

Who it's for

Security engineers, penetration testers and consultants who need an on-premises scanner. Essentials fits short evaluations and home labs, and Essentials Plus is free for verified students. Expert fits teams that also scan web apps.

Standout features

  • Vulnerability scoring with CVSS v4, EPSS and Tenable's VPR.
  • Configuration audits against CIS benchmarks with pre-built compliance policies.
  • Live Results reassesses past scan data offline after every plugin update.
  • More than 450 pre-configured scan templates.
  • Expert adds web app scanning and external attack surface discovery.

Integrations

Tenable documents a migration path to its other products. Nessus product pages list no third-party ticketing or SIEM integrations directly.

Pros / cons

  • Pro: Large plugin library with over 319K plugins.
  • Pro: Unlimited scanning on a flat annual Professional license.
  • Pro: Free 30-day Essentials license for learning and testing.
  • Pro: Runs on VMs and even Raspberry Pi.
  • Con: Commercial use starts at $4,790 per year for Professional.
  • Con: SSO is not documented on Nessus pages.
  • Con: Essentials caps scans at 5 IPs, runs on a 30-day license and gets plugin updates on a 30-day delay.
  • Con: Web app scans in Expert start at 5 FQDNs.

FAQ

FAQs about Tenable Nessus

Is Nessus free?

Nessus Essentials is a free 30-day license for non-commercial use that scans up to 5 IPs. Essentials Plus costs $199 per year and is free for verified students and instructors.

How much does Nessus Professional cost?

Tenable lists Nessus Professional at $4,790 for a one-year license. Two-year and three-year terms are also sold at a lower yearly rate.

What is the difference between Nessus Professional and Expert?

Expert includes every Professional feature and adds web app scanning and external attack surface discovery. It costs $6,790 for a one-year license.

Can Nessus run on a virtual machine?

Yes. Tenable's documentation says Nessus can be installed on a virtual machine that meets the same hardware requirements as a physical host.

Does Nessus check compliance?

Yes. Professional includes pre-built policies for configuration and compliance audits, including checks against CIS benchmarks.
