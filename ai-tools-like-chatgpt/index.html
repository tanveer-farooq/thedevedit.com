Microsoft Copilot

Best overall

Microsoft Copilot is Microsoft's AI assistant, free with a signed-in Microsoft account, with paid tiers sold as Microsoft 365 plans.

9.5/10 Visit Microsoft Copilot

Microsoft has renamed its work app: "The Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat app is now called Microsoft Copilot Chat."

Why we picked it

Copilot is the only tool here with full marks for ChatGPT jobs, free and entry access, and integrations. Microsoft lists "over 100 prebuilt connectors" for data beyond Microsoft 365, and Copilot Chat comes "included at no additional cost with eligible Microsoft 365 plans".

Who it's for

Companies on Microsoft 365, and people who already pay for Microsoft 365 Personal, Family or Premium.

Standout features

  • Copilot Chat: at work it answers from the web and from files you upload with a prompt.
  • Microsoft Graph grounding: the Microsoft 365 Copilot add-on answers from your emails, files, meetings and calendars.
  • Copilot Vision: gives "visual AI assistance from your screen or camera" on paid Microsoft 365 plans.
  • Researcher and Analyst: research and data-analysis agents with "Extensive usage" on Premium and Pro.

Integrations

Integrations include Salesforce, ServiceNow, Jira, Confluence, Google Drive, Box and Workday, through Microsoft 365 Copilot connectors.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026: a limited free version, Microsoft 365 Personal at $9.99/mo ($99.99/yr), and the Copilot Business add-on at $21/user/mo billed yearly.

Pros

  • With Entra ID, prompts and responses "aren't used to train foundation models."
  • Signing in to the free app adds voice interactions.
  • Custom connectors can be built with the Microsoft 365 Agents Toolkit.

Cons

  • Researcher and Analyst are "Not available" on Personal or Family.
  • Copilot Vision "is only available during a voice conversation."
  • Family: Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook is owner-only.

Claude

Best standalone assistant

Claude is Anthropic's AI assistant, and unlike ChatGPT Enterprise, every Claude plan, Enterprise included, has a published price.

9.5/10 Visit Claude

Why we picked it

Claude scores full marks for ChatGPT jobs, integrations and admin controls. Its connectors directory lists "over 950 MCP servers", and Team includes SSO with "No model training on your content by default."

Who it's for

Individuals and teams of 2 to 150 who want an assistant outside the Microsoft and Google ecosystems.

Standout features

  • Code execution: from Free up, Claude can "Create and edit files with code execution."
  • Spreadsheet uploads: with code execution on, you can upload XLSX files alongside PDF, DOCX and CSV.
  • Voice mode: spoken conversations on every plan, which Anthropic still labels "a beta feature."
  • Web search and Research: web search runs on every plan; the Research feature starts on Pro.

Integrations

Integrations include Slack, Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, Linear and Microsoft 365, through Claude connectors.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free is $0, Pro is $17/mo billed annually or $20 monthly, and Team Standard is $20/seat/mo annually or $25 monthly.

Pros

  • Anthropic lists ISO 27001, ISO/IEC 42001 and SOC 2.
  • Uploads allow 500 MB per file and 20 files per chat.
  • The free plan includes up to 5 projects.

Cons

  • SCIM, audit logs and retention controls are Enterprise-only.
  • Free, Pro, Max and Ultra list model training as "Opt-out."
  • Enterprise needs 20 seats and adds usage at API rates.

Gemini

Best low-cost upgrade

Gemini is Google's AI assistant, with the lowest paid entry on this list: Google AI Plus at $4.99 a month as of Nov 2026.

9.4/10 Visit Gemini

Why we picked it

Gemini scores full marks for ChatGPT jobs, free access and admin controls. Google's plans page lists Deep Research and Gemini Live on the free plan, and says Gemini Enterprise data "isn't used to train Google models."

Who it's for

Companies on Google Workspace, and individuals already using Gmail, Google Drive and an Android phone.

Standout features

  • Gemini Live: "a natural, free-flowing conversation with Gemini", with camera and screen sharing on Android.
  • Spreadsheet charts: ask the web app to "create a chart based on your uploaded spreadsheet(s)."
  • Large uploads: up to 10 files per prompt, 100 MB each, or 2 GB per video.

Integrations

Integrations include Microsoft SharePoint, OneDrive, Outlook, Jira Cloud, Confluence Cloud and ServiceNow, through Gemini Enterprise connectors.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026: Free, Google AI Plus $4.99/mo, Google AI Pro $19.99/mo and Ultra from $99.99/mo; Gemini Enterprise starts at $21/seat/mo, billing term not stated.

Pros

  • Workspace admins can auto-delete chats, from 90 days to about 3 years.
  • Canvas and image generation are included on the free plan.
  • Google AI Plus costs less than ChatGPT Go's $8 a month.

Cons

  • Gemini Live "isn't available in the Gemini web app."
  • Below AI Pro, video uploads cap at 5 minutes, audio at 10.
  • Going past plan limits means buying AI credits.

Perplexity

Best for cited research

Perplexity is an AI assistant that answers from the live web and puts "Citations in every answer", free plan included.

9.2/10 Visit Perplexity

Why we picked it

Perplexity scores full marks for integrations and admin controls. It supports custom remote connectors over MCP, and Perplexity Enterprise lists SSO, SCIM, audit logs and automatic file deletion.

Who it's for

Analysts, product managers and sales teams who need answers they can check against the original sources.

Standout features

  • Voice mode: "a natural, back-and-forth conversation with Perplexity while it works for you", on Pro and Max.
  • Model choice: on Pro you can "Select between AI models" for each query.
  • Uploads: text, code, PDFs, images, audio and video, up to 40 MB per file.
  • Computer: available on paid plans, it "writes docs, builds slides, sends emails, browses the web."

Integrations

Integrations include Google Drive, Gmail, Outlook, SharePoint, Slack, Notion and HubSpot, through Perplexity's connectors.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026: Free $0, Pro $20/mo or $200/yr, Max $200/mo, Enterprise Pro $40/seat/mo and Enterprise Max $325/seat/mo.

Pros

  • Perplexity states: "We never train our LLMs on enterprise customer's data."
  • Perplexity's security page says it is "SOC 2 Type 2 certified."
  • On Mac, voice mode "can also start work on your own computer."

Cons

  • We found no Perplexity page on spreadsheet or data analysis.
  • Voice mode is "not available in mobile browsers."
  • Credits on top of subscriptions make heavy use harder to forecast.

Grok

Best for X and web search

Grok is an AI assistant with "direct access to X and the web", plus voice conversations, deep research and document analysis.

9.1/10 Visit Grok

Grok's App Store listing and docs now name its developer as SpaceXSI. xAI's consumer FAQ and privacy policy didn't load for our review, so we couldn't confirm a chat retention setting.

Why we picked it

Grok earns full marks for free and entry access, and xAI's docs say "Connectors are available to all Grok users." You can add your own MCP server as a custom connector if it is reachable over the public internet.

Who it's for

People who follow news and markets on X, and teams that want Grok to search their Google Drive.

Standout features

  • One usage pool: The X Subscription shares usage between Grok and Cursor.
  • Drive permissions: xAI says "Grok respects your existing Google Drive permissions."
  • Enterprise controls: "Directory Sync (SCIM)", "Advanced audit and security controls" and a Vault add-on with customer-managed keys.

Integrations

Integrations include Gmail, Google Drive, OneDrive, Outlook, Microsoft Teams, SharePoint and Salesforce, through Grok's built-in connectors.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, The X Subscription runs Free, Plus $8/mo, Premium $30/mo, Super $100/mo and Ultra $200/mo; team seats start at $30.

Pros

  • xAI's security FAQ states: "We are SOC 2 Type 2 compliant."
  • xAI (Dec 2025) promises Grok Business data "no training on it, ever."
  • Existing SuperGrok and X Premium plans continue unless you switch.

Cons

  • The iOS app still sells SuperGrok tiers from $10 to $300.
  • SSO is Enterprise-only, and "Organizations are exclusive to the Enterprise tier."
  • We found no spreadsheet or data-analysis feature documented for Grok.

Mistral Vibe

Best for self-hosted deployment

Mistral Vibe, formerly Le Chat, is Mistral AI's assistant, and the only one here whose Enterprise plan can run self-hosted or on-premises.

8.5/10 Visit Mistral Vibe

Why we picked it

Mistral Vibe scores full marks for integrations: its pricing page lists "100+ connectors" on the free plan and says Vibe can "Connect to any remote MCP server of choice" (beta). Admins can disable specific connectors for the whole organization.

Who it's for

Companies that need an assistant inside their own environment, and teams that want to share reusable instructions across a workspace.

Standout features

  • Skills: "reusable instructions and resources" in a SKILL.md file; deep research ships as a built-in Skill.
  • Cited web answers: "Every web-sourced response comes with its references."
  • Code interpreter: on paid plans, it works through CSV, XLSX and JSON files and draws charts.
  • Image input: Vibe can "interpret photos, diagrams, screenshots, and scanned pages" on every plan.
  • Enterprise admin: SAML SSO and "Detailed logs for tracking usage, changes, and ensuring compliance."

Integrations

Integrations include Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, Notion, Outlook, SharePoint and Linear, through Vibe connectors.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026: Free, Pro $14.99/mo, Team $24.99/user/mo and Enterprise by sales; PAYG credits at API rates extend paid plans past their limits.

Pros

  • Verified students pay $5.99/mo for up to 12 months.
  • Mistral says it complies with SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001/27701.
  • "Enterprise customers are opted out of training by default."

Cons

  • Voice mode transcribes speech; we found no spoken replies.
  • Vibe chats "are used by default to train" unless you opt out.
  • SCIM is "available in private preview", and we found no retention control.

Duck.ai

Best for private chat

Duck.ai is DuckDuckGo's free AI chat for "private conversations with 3rd-party AI chat models, anonymized by us."

8.3/10 Visit Duck.ai

Why we picked it

Duck.ai earns full marks for free access and pricing clarity, and covers five of ChatGPT's six jobs on our count. Conversations are "not used to train chat models" by DuckDuckGo or the model providers.

Who it's for

Individuals who want ChatGPT-style chat with models from several labs, with chats kept on their own device by default.

Standout features

  • Anonymization: DuckDuckGo removes "Metadata that contains personal information (for example, your IP address)."
  • Provider deletion: model providers agree to "Delete your prompts and responses immediately after generating a reply."
  • Voice chat: it "generates both a spoken and text response", and audio isn't stored by DuckDuckGo or OpenAI.
  • Model choice: free models include ones from OpenAI, Anthropic and Mistral AI; Pro adds Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-5.6 Sol.

Integrations

We found no third-party connectors or integrations in DuckDuckGo's Duck.ai help pages.

Pricing

Free with a daily limit; as of Nov 2026, DuckDuckGo Plus is $9.99/mo and Pro $19.99/mo, per its March 2026 announcement.

Pros

  • "You can disable chat history at any time in Duck.ai settings."
  • Chats started from DuckDuckGo's homepage can use web search and image creation.
  • Optional Sync & Backup is end-to-end encrypted; DuckDuckGo "cannot read your chats."

Cons

  • Uploads take images and PDFs on some models only, not spreadsheets.
  • Exceptions allow legally required retention and up to 1 hour in memory.
  • We found no business plan, SSO or audit logs.

FAQ

FAQs about AI tools like ChatGPT

What is the best free AI tool like ChatGPT?

Claude and Gemini both document all six ChatGPT jobs on their free plans, within usage limits. Meta AI's App Store listing also describes all six in a free app, but we found no business controls for it. Our free AI tools list goes wider.

What happened to Mistral Le Chat?

Le Chat is now Mistral Vibe. Mistral renamed it on May 28, 2026 and says your plan, history and settings carry over at the same address, chat.mistral.ai. Mistral says "We will soon sunset Chat mode" and that Le Chat history stays in Work Mode, so build new workflows there.

Which AI tool like ChatGPT is best for beginners?

Start with the assistant tied to an account you already have: Microsoft Copilot with a Microsoft account, or Gemini with a Google account. Both have free tiers, so you can learn the basics before paying. At work, ask IT first, because Copilot Chat may already be included in your Microsoft 365 plan.

Can AI chatbots like ChatGPT replace a search engine?

For many questions they answer from the live web, but you still need to check sources. Microsoft Copilot can retrieve information from the Bing search service, and Perplexity and Mistral Vibe attach sources to web answers. Brave's own Leo page advises checking answers before relying on them, and an answer with no link should be treated as unverified.

ChatGPT or Claude?

Both document all six ChatGPT jobs, and both sell business plans at $20 per seat per month billed annually. ChatGPT's Go plan costs $8 a month but OpenAI says it "may include ads", while Claude Pro costs $17 a month billed annually.
