# Jamf review

Jamf makes device management and security software for Apple fleets only. This review is docs-based: we scored it from Jamf's product, pricing and trust pages, with no hands-on testing.

By TheDevEdit Editorial Team · Docs-based review · Prices checked Nov 15, 2026

OVERVIEW
THE SHORT ANSWER

Jamf is a strong pick for IT teams that run Mac, iPhone and iPad fleets and want management, security and identity from one Apple-focused vendor. Its paid bundles start at $5.75 per mobile device a month, but it does not manage Windows or Android.

OUR VERDICT

If your company runs only Apple devices, start your shortlist with Jamf. Its Mac bundle documents management, endpoint security and Zero Trust access in one price, but Microsoft Intune is the better fit for mixed fleets.

Jamf's about page says "He co-founded Jamf in 2000" and that "More than 76,600 organizations rely on Jamf." The same page says "Jamf manages and secures more than 33.6 million devices."

A May 2026 Jamf release notes the move to private ownership "following Francisco Partners' acquisition, completed in January 2026."

WHAT WE LIKED

  • \+ Zero-touch setup: Jamf Pro can "Provision Mac, iPhone, iPad or Apple TV with hands-free zero-touch deployment for your users"
  • \+ Self Service+, where "Users can install apps, update software and maintain their own devices."
  • \+ Jamf for Mac bundles Jamf Pro, Jamf Connect and Jamf Protect in one per-device price
  • \+ The trust center lists "certifications like SOC 2, ISO 27001 and StateRAMP"

WHAT HELD IT BACK

  • – Apple only: the product pages name Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, with no Windows or Android
  • – Jamf for Mac and Jamf for Mobile are billed annually with a 25-device minimum
  • – Jamf School has no published price on the pricing page
  • – The $4 Jamf Now tier lists malware protection as its only named security feature

KEY FEATURES

Device management. Jamf Pro's page says "Jamf Pro delivers automated Apple device management and security, helping IT teams configure, protect, and patch devices." It also says Jamf Pro can "patch all your Apple devices without needing user interaction."

Endpoint security. Jamf describes Jamf Protect as "best-in-class Apple endpoint security for work." On the Mac bundle it covers "Endpoint protection, vulnerability management and content filtering."

Identity and sign-in. Jamf Connect "provides frictionless Mac authentication and secure access to everything users need with a single cloud sign-on."

Small business and schools. Jamf Now lets you "Manage up to 3 devices for free." Jamf School "offers an easy MDM for school devices" and targets K-12.

Integrations: Apple, Google, Microsoft, Okta and ServiceNow appear on Jamf's integrations page, which says "Visit the Jamf Marketplace for a full listing of partners and integrations available with Jamf."

FAQ

FAQs about Jamf

Does Jamf manage Windows or Android devices?

No, not on the pages we checked. Jamf's product pages name only Apple devices, so mixed fleets usually pair it with, or choose, a cross-platform tool such as Microsoft Intune.

Is there a free version of Jamf?

Yes, for very small fleets. Jamf Now lets you "Manage up to 3 devices for free," and paid Jamf Now starts at $4 per device a month (as of Nov 2026).

What is the difference between Jamf Pro and Jamf for Mac?

Jamf Pro is the management product, and Jamf for Mac is a priced bundle. The pricing page says Jamf for Mac is powered by "Jamf Pro, Jamf Connect and Jamf Protect."

Does Jamf publish security certifications?

Yes. Jamf's trust center lists "certifications like SOC 2, ISO 27001 and StateRAMP," and we found no FedRAMP claim on that page.
