MD

Microsoft Defender for Endpoint

Best for Microsoft 365 shops

Microsoft Defender for Endpoint is a mobile threat defense app for Android and iOS that feeds risk signals into Intune and Conditional Access.

9.5/10 Visit Microsoft Defender for Endpoint

Why we picked it

It scores full marks on threat coverage, deployment and pricing clarity. The MTD docs cover phishing, rogue Wi-Fi, jailbreak detection, malware scanning and OS and app vulnerability assessment, and Defender for Business lists a price and a one-month trial.

Who it's for

Security teams already on Microsoft 365 and Intune that want phone risk to block access to work apps.

Standout features

  • Web protection: "Anti-phishing, blocking unsafe network connections", plus custom URL and domain indicators.
  • Vulnerability management: "OS and Apps vulnerabilities assessment for both Android and iOS".
  • Conditional Access: "Blocking risky devices from accessing corporate resources."
  • BYOD coverage: supports Android work profiles on personally owned devices and app-only MAM protection.
  • Government clouds: documented endpoints for GCC, GCC High and DoD environments.

Integrations

Connects to Microsoft Intune, Microsoft Tunnel and the unified Microsoft Defender portal; MAM also covers devices "enrolled with non-Microsoft Enterprise Mobility Management".

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Defender for Business is $3.00 per user/month, paid yearly, up to 300 users, with a one-month trial. Intune is licensed separately.

Pros

  • Published $3.00 per user price with a one-month trial.
  • Vulnerability assessment covers both Android and iOS.
  • US government cloud options are documented.

Cons

  • Intune is a separate product and licence.
  • Malware scanning is Android-only.
  • We found no SIEM export on its MTD pages.

Zimperium

Best for on-prem and FedRAMP

Zimperium is a mobile threat defense platform for Android, iOS and ChromeOS that runs in the cloud, on-premises or air-gapped.

8.9/10 Visit Zimperium

Why we picked it

Zimperium scores full marks on threat coverage, platform coverage and deployment. Its MTD page covers phishing, rogue networks, jailbreaks, risky apps and vulnerabilities, and it "Supports cloud, on-premises, air-gapped and FedRamp".

Who it's for

Government and regulated teams that need mobile threat defense hosted on their own servers or air-gapped.

Standout features

  • Device integrity: "Detects jailbreaks, rooting, and other compromises before granting access to corporate resources."
  • App risk: "Assesses security, permissions, compliance, and malware risks for enterprise and personal apps."
  • Network threats: "Detect unsafe and rogue networks."
  • ChromeOS support: covers "Android, iOS, and ChromeOS", including Chromebooks.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Zimperium publishes no price and no free trial on its MTD page; pricing comes through sales.

Pros

  • Only tool here with documented on-prem and air-gapped options.
  • Covers ChromeOS as well as Android and iOS.
  • Feeds MDM, SIEM, SOAR and XDR platforms.

Cons

  • No published price or free trial.
  • We found no unified console with desktops in its docs.
  • Its MTD page names no specific integration partners.

Sophos Mobile

Best for MDM plus MTD

Sophos Mobile is device management and mobile threat defense under one licence, covering iOS, iPadOS, Android and ChromeOS.

8.6/10 Visit Sophos Mobile

Sophos takes full platform marks, pairs MDM with MTD and feeds Sophos XDR. It "combines MDM and MTD in a single product experience and a single Sophos Mobile license" and offers a 30-day trial.

Who it's for

IT teams that want to manage devices and block mobile threats from one product.

Standout features

  • Threat defense: it guards users from "phishing, malicious or unwanted apps, unsafe websites, and risky networks."
  • BYOD programs: it manages corporate-owned, personally enabled and BYOD devices, keeping business data separate.
  • Compliance actions: it can "automate policy-based action when devices fall out of compliance."
  • Broad OS support: iOS, iPadOS and Android phones, plus Windows, macOS and ChromeOS computers.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Sophos publishes no price; you request a quote through Get pricing. A free 30-day trial is available.

Pros

  • MDM and MTD come in one licence.
  • Covers iOS, iPadOS, Android and ChromeOS.
  • Free 30-day trial, plus Conditional Access and XDR feeds.

Cons

  • We found no published price on its product page.
  • We found no jailbreak or root detection in its docs.
  • We found no government or air-gapped option in its docs.

FAQ

FAQs about mobile security software

What is mobile security software?

It is software that protects company phones and tablets from phishing, rogue Wi-Fi, jailbroken or rooted devices and risky apps. Most tools here are mobile threat defense apps that pass device risk to an MDM, so unsafe phones can be blocked from work resources.

What is the best mobile security software?

Microsoft Defender for Endpoint scores highest on our rubric at 9.5/10. It covers all five threat areas, supports BYOD through Android work profiles and MAM, and Defender for Business lists a $3 per user monthly price with a one-month trial.

Which mobile security tools have published prices?

Three do as of Nov 2026: Microsoft Defender for Business at $3 per user a month, CrowdStrike Falcon Go at $59.99 per device a year, and Jamf for Mobile at $5.75 per device a month. Zimperium, Ivanti, Sophos and Check Point sell through sales.

Which mobile security tool can run on-premises?

Zimperium is the only tool on this list that documents on-premises and air-gapped options. Its MTD page says it supports cloud, on-premises, air-gapped and FedRAMP deployments, and it covers ChromeOS as well as Android and iOS.

Do I need MDM to use mobile threat defense?

Usually yes. Microsoft Defender works with Intune, Ivanti is built to run with Ivanti MDM, and CrowdStrike deploys through your existing UEM or MDM. Sophos and Jamf bundle MDM with threat defense, so you buy both from one vendor.
