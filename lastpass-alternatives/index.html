# The 7 best LastPass alternatives for 2026

LastPass limits its free plan to one device type, and its pricing page loads prices only by script. We ranked 7 alternatives from vendor docs and pricing pages on one published rubric.

TheDevEdit Editorial Team, Editorial team · 11 min read · 7 alternatives reviewed · prices checked Nov 15, 2026

THE SHORT ANSWER

Bitwarden is the best LastPass alternative on our rubric (9.5/10). It is open source, publishes every paid price, and adds SCIM, event logs and a self-hosting option for businesses.

Pick Passbolt to run the whole server yourself, or KeePassXC for an offline vault.

  • BEST SELF-HOSTED · Pb Passbolt · Free Community Edition for unlimited users on your server. · Visit site ↗
  • BEST OFFLINE · Kx KeePassXC · Free desktop vault kept in one encrypted file. · Visit site ↗
WHY TEAMS SWITCH

Why teams look for a LastPass alternative

  • Free plan limits: LastPass Free is "Limited to 1 device type."
  • Team size cap: the LastPass pricing page lists "Teams Up to 50 users", so larger teams move to Business.
  • SSO apps capped: unlimited SSO on Business Max is "in addition to the three included in the base Business plan".
  • No self-hosting: we found no self-hosted LastPass option on its pricing page, which some security teams require.

The 7 best LastPass alternatives, reviewed

#1 Bw

Bitwarden

Best open source for most teams · 9.5 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Bitwarden ↗

Bitwarden is an open-source password manager with a free plan, low personal prices and business plans that can self-host.

Why we picked it

It takes full marks on security and admin controls. Its pricing page lists "Open source security Zero-knowledge encryption" in every account.

Who it's for

Individuals and businesses that want open-source code and published prices on every paid plan.

Standout features

  • Event logs: Teams lets admins "Audit activity with event logs".
  • Provisioning: Teams can "Automate provisioning with SCIM".
  • Self-hosting: Enterprise adds "Flexibility to self-host".
  • Bitwarden Send: it can "Transmit data securely to anyone, even non-users, with end-to-end encryption."

Integrations

Bitwarden supports "Organization two-step login via Duo" and SSO on Enterprise.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Premium is $1.65/mo, Families $3.99/mo, Teams $4 and Enterprise $6 per user per month, billed annually.

Pros

  • A free plan: "Get basic password management today. Always free."
  • It "holds certifications such as ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, and SOC 3".
  • Every paid price is printed on the page.

Cons

  • SSO and self-hosting sit on Enterprise, not Teams.
  • Large organizations get a sales-led custom plan.

Switch if

You want open-source code, SCIM and event logs at a per-user price you can read before talking to sales.

Stay with lastpass if

Your team uses LastPass SaaS Monitoring & Protect, which Bitwarden's pricing pages do not list.

#2 Pb

Passbolt

Best self-hosted team vaults · 8.3 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Passbolt ↗

Passbolt is an open-source team password manager that you run on your own server or buy as a managed cloud.

Why we picked it

It takes full marks on admin controls and deployment. Community Edition is free "With unlimited users Open source under AGPLV3 license".

Who it's for

IT and DevOps teams that must keep the password server inside their own network.

Standout features

  • Directory sync: Pro adds "Ldap provisioning (AD / OpenLdap) Single Sign On (SSO) with Microsoft, Google & OpenID".
  • Audit trail: Pro includes an "Activity log (audit changes)".
  • Developer access: every edition has "Browser extensions & CLI Open API Role Based Access Control".

Integrations

It installs as a "Turn-key installer Docker container Virtual Machine" and supports kdbx and CSV import.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Community is free and Pro is "$4.9 /month per user Billed annually Minimum 10 users." Enterprise is by quote.

Pros

  • Free edition has no user limit.
  • Import and export cover "Import / export (kdbx, csv)".
  • Role-based access control is in the free edition.

Cons

  • Pro needs at least 10 users.
  • We found no third-party certification on the fetched pricing page.

Switch if

Your policy says credentials must live on servers you control, with LDAP and SSO.

Stay with lastpass if

You want a vendor-run cloud for personal use with no server to maintain.

#3 Ke

Keeper

Best certified enterprise vaults · 8.2 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Keeper ↗

Keeper is a zero-knowledge password manager with business, enterprise and privileged access products.

Why we picked it

It has the broadest certification list we found. Keeper's business pricing page lists "ISO 27001, 27017 and 27018 certified".

Who it's for

Mid-size and large companies that need audit evidence for vendor security reviews.

Standout features

  • Business plan: "Includes encrypted vaults, shared team folders, role-based policies and basic two-factor authentication."
  • Enterprise plan: "Adds advanced features like Single Sign-On (SAML 2.0), SCIM provisioning and advanced MFA options."
  • SIEM feed: admins can "feed live event data into external SIEM products".

Integrations

Its product list includes "Self-Managed Keeper" and a Secrets Manager.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Keeper Business and Enterprise are per user per month, billed annually; the page loads figures by script.

Pros

  • It is "SOC 2 Type 2 and SOC 3 certified".
  • Personal plans list "Unlimited devices and sync Secure record sharing Emergency access".

Cons

  • We found no free plan on the fetched pages.
  • Prices were not in the page text we fetched.
  • The code is not open source.

Switch if

You need SOC 2, ISO certificates and a SIEM event feed for compliance reviews.

Stay with lastpass if

You need a free tier, which we did not find on Keeper's pages.

#4 En

Enpass

Best for vaults in your own cloud storage · 8.1 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Enpass ↗

Enpass is a password manager that syncs your vault through cloud storage you already use, not its own servers.

Why we picked it

It takes full marks on data control and entry price. Enpass says "Every plan keeps your vault in your own iCloud, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Box, or other personal cloud".

Who it's for

People and small teams who want no vendor-held copy of their vault.

Standout features

  • Authenticator: an "Integrated authenticator With one-time codes".
  • Business admin: the Enpass site lists "Admin Console SCIM provisioning SIEM integrations Policy enforcement".
  • Breach alerts: breach and dark web monitoring on paid plans.

Integrations

Apps run on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android, with browser extensions, per the Enpass pricing page.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free lets you "Store up to 25 items in 1 vault"; Premium is $1.99/mo and Family $3.99/mo, billed annually.

Pros

  • Low personal price.
  • Vault data stays off Enpass servers.
  • Linux desktop app.

Cons

  • Free caps you at 25 items.
  • We found no SSO on the fetched page.
  • Business prices were not in the page text.

Switch if

You want your vault synced through your own cloud drive rather than a vendor server.

Stay with lastpass if

You want SSO for the vault, which the Enpass pricing page does not list.

#5 Kx

KeePassXC

Best offline personal vault · 8.0 /10 (editorial score) · Visit KeePassXC ↗

KeePassXC is a free, open-source desktop password manager that keeps your vault in one encrypted file.

Why we picked it

It takes full marks on security, deployment and price. Its homepage says "The source code is completely open source under the GPLv3 license".

Who it's for

Developers and privacy-minded users who want no account and no cloud service.

Standout features

  • Offline file: data sits "in an offline, encrypted file that can be stored in any location".
  • Certified: "KeePassXC 2.7.9 has been awarded a security Visa by the French National Cybersecurity Agency (ANSSI)."
  • Active releases: the project blog posted "KeePassXC 2.8.0 (Stable) released" in October 2026.

Integrations

It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux, and many Linux distributions package it, per KeePassXC.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, it is free: "No cloud, no ads, no subscriptions."

Pros

  • No vendor holds your data.
  • Free with no paid tier.
  • Independent ANSSI certification.

Cons

  • No SSO, provisioning or admin console.
  • You handle sync and backups yourself.

Switch if

You manage your own passwords alone and want an offline vault with no subscription.

Stay with lastpass if

You share credentials across a team and need central admin controls.

FAQ

FAQs about LastPass alternatives for

What is the best free LastPass alternative?

Bitwarden, for most people. Every account gets "Unlimited devices, unlimited passwords", and KeePassXC is fully free if you want an offline vault.

Is there a self-hosted LastPass alternative?

Yes: Passbolt Community Edition is free to self-host with unlimited users. Bitwarden Enterprise and RoboForm Enterprise (1,000+ users) also document self-hosting.

Which LastPass alternative is open source?

Bitwarden, Passbolt and KeePassXC publish their source code. KeePassXC uses GPLv3 and Passbolt uses AGPLv3.

Which alternative has the most security certifications?

Keeper lists SOC 2 Type 2, SOC 3 and three ISO certificates on its business pricing page. Bitwarden lists ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II and SOC 3.
