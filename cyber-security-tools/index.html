Wa No. 01Best open source SIEM and XDR

Wazuh

 8.7/10

Wazuh is a free, open-source SIEM and XDR platform you self-host, or run on Wazuh Cloud from $571 a month.

Visit Wazuh

Why we picked it

It is the only free, open-source SIEM and XDR here. Its platform overview documents vulnerability detection and compliance reports for PCI DSS, NIST, TSC and HIPAA.

Who it's for

Security teams that want SIEM and XDR without license fees and can run their own servers.

Standout features

  • Vulnerability detection: agents match software inventory against CVE data to "identify known vulnerable software."
  • Compliance reports: "reports and dashboards for regulations such as PCI DSS, NIST, TSC, and HIPAA."
  • Active response: "out-of-the-box active responses to perform various countermeasures against ongoing threats."
  • File integrity monitoring: tracks changes to file content, permissions, ownership and attributes.

Integrations

Integrations include AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Microsoft 365 and Docker, per Wazuh's platform overview.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, self-hosted Wazuh is free; Wazuh Cloud starts at $571/month, with Medium $923, Large $1,467 and Custom by quote.

Pros

  • Free and open source.
  • Cloud plans have a 14-day trial, no card.
  • Compliance dashboards for PCI DSS and HIPAA.

Cons

  • Wazuh Cloud starts at $571 a month.
  • Self-hosting means running the indexer cluster yourself.
  • We found no audit log details in its docs.

No. 02Best for team password management

Bitwarden

 8.5/10

Bitwarden is a password manager with an always-free account, a $4 Teams plan, event logs, and self-hosting on Enterprise.

Visit Bitwarden

Why we picked it

It scores full marks on free and entry access. The business pricing page pairs an always-free account with a $4 Teams plan and documents SSO and event logs.

Who it's for

Teams that need shared credential vaults, with an option to run the server themselves.

Standout features

  • Event logs: "View detailed records of over 50 types of events with timestamps."
  • Self-host option: Enterprise lets you "Run the Bitwarden server application on your own network or server."
  • SCIM provisioning: both business plans list automated provisioning with SCIM and a Directory Connector.
  • Passwordless SSO: Enterprise connects to your existing identity provider.

Integrations

Integrations include Duo and Yubico OTP for two-factor authentication, per Bitwarden's business pricing page.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, the account is free, Teams is $4 per user/month billed annually, and Enterprise is $6.

Pros

  • Always-free account for basic password management.
  • Teams costs $4 a user.
  • Self-hosting available on Enterprise.

Cons

  • SSO and self-hosting need Enterprise.
  • Business plans bill annually only.
  • We found no threat detection or automated response on its pricing page.

MD No. 03Best for Microsoft 365 shops

Microsoft Defender for Business

 8.0/10

Microsoft Defender for Business is endpoint security for firms up to 300 users, with EDR and automated remediation for $3 a user.

Visit Microsoft Defender for Business

Why we picked it

It is the only tool here with all five core capability elements. The overview docs list next-generation protection, EDR, automated investigation and remediation, monthly security reports and APIs.

Who it's for

Small and mid-size businesses on Microsoft 365, and the MSPs that manage them.

Standout features

  • Automated investigation and remediation: included in Defender for Business, but not in Defender for Endpoint Plan 1.
  • Automatic attack disruption: listed in the feature table alongside optimized EDR.
  • Cross-platform devices: it covers Windows "and with apps for Mac, iOS, and Android devices."
  • Multitenant management: MSPs get Microsoft Defender multitenant management and Microsoft 365 Lighthouse views.

Integrations

Integrations include Microsoft Intune, Microsoft 365 Lighthouse, and MSP RMM and PSA tools, per Microsoft's overview docs.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Defender for Business is $3.00 per user/month, paid yearly; Microsoft 365 Business Premium, which includes it, is $22.00.

Pros

  • $3 a user covers EDR and automated remediation.
  • Bundled with Microsoft 365 Business Premium.
  • Built-in multitenant management for MSPs.

Cons

  • Capped at 300 users.
  • Servers need extra licenses.
  • Annual billing only.

CrowdStrike Falcon

 7.1/10

CrowdStrike Falcon is a cloud endpoint protection platform that bundles next-gen antivirus, EDR and threat hunting for Windows, macOS and Linux.

Visit CrowdStrike Falcon

Falcon scores 8 on core capability. Even the entry Falcon Go bundle lists next-gen antivirus, EDR and threat intelligence and hunting, from $7.99 per device a month.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Falcon Go is $7.99 per device monthly, Pro $14.99, Enterprise $19.99; Complete is by sales.

Okta

 7.1/10

Okta is a cloud identity platform that gives employees single sign-on and MFA, with a free 10-user plan and paid Starter seats.

Visit Okta

Okta takes full marks on entry access. The Integrator Free Plan covers up to 10 active users, and Starter costs $6 per user a month.

IT teams that need one login and MFA across workforce apps without running identity servers.

Standout features

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Starter is $6 and Essentials $17 per user monthly, billed annually; Professional and Enterprise are by inquiry.

Cons

Burp Suite

 6.4/10

Burp Suite is PortSwigger's web app testing toolkit, pairing an intercepting proxy and manual tools with a web vulnerability scanner.

Visit Burp Suite

Why we picked it

Burp Suite covers manual and automated web app testing in one tool. The free Community Edition includes the proxy and core manual tools, and Pro is $499.

Who it's for

Pentesters and AppSec engineers who test web apps and APIs by hand.

Standout features

Pricing

Cons

FAQs about cyber security tools

What are cyber security tools?

Cyber security tools are software that protects devices, accounts, networks and apps from attack. This list covers endpoint protection, password management, SIEM and XDR, identity and SSO, vulnerability scanning and web app pentesting.

Which cyber security tools are free?

Five of the seven have a free option. Bitwarden has a free account, Wazuh is open source, Okta's Integrator plan covers 10 users, Nessus Essentials scans 5 IPs for non-commercial use, and Burp Suite has a free Community edition.

What is the best cyber security tool for a small business?

Wazuh scores highest on our rubric and is free to self-host. If you have no servers to run, Microsoft Defender for Business costs $3 per user a month, paid yearly, covers firms up to 300 users, and includes EDR and automated investigation and remediation.

Which tool is best for open-source security monitoring?

Wazuh. It is a free, open-source SIEM and XDR platform you self-host, with vulnerability detection and compliance reports for PCI DSS, NIST, TSC and HIPAA. Wazuh Cloud starts at $571 a month.
