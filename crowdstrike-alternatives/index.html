# The 7 best CrowdStrike alternatives for 2026

CrowdStrike Falcon is a cloud endpoint platform with published per-device prices, but its device caps and sales-only MDR tier send some teams looking. We ranked 7 alternatives from vendor docs and pricing pages on the rubric we use for endpoint security software.

TheDevEdit Editorial Team, Editorial team · 11 min read · 7 alternatives reviewed · prices checked Nov 15, 2026

THE SHORT ANSWER

Microsoft Defender for Business is the best CrowdStrike alternative for most small teams on our rubric (8.1/10, against Falcon's 8.5). It costs $3 per user a month and covers up to five devices per user.

Pick Sophos for ransomware rollback or SentinelOne for full EDR controls.

WHY TEAMS SWITCH

Why teams look for a CrowdStrike Falcon alternative

  • Device cap on the cheapest bundle: CrowdStrike's pricing page says "Purchases of Falcon Go are limited to a maximum of 100 devices", so growing fleets move up a tier.
  • Price per device adds up: Falcon Enterprise lists "$184.99 per device, billed annually", well above per-user bundles from Microsoft.
  • Managed service is quote-only: Falcon Complete MDR is shown as "Contact sales" with no public price.
  • Short trial scope: the trial "provides access to CrowdStrike Falcon® Prevent (next-gen antivirus)" for 15 days, not the full EDR bundle.

#1 Md

Microsoft Defender for Business

Best for Microsoft 365 shops · 8.1 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Microsoft Defender ↗

Microsoft Defender for Business is EDR and next-gen antivirus for firms of up to 300 users, sold per user rather than per device.

Why we picked it

It has the lowest published entry price here and no device minimum. Its product page lists "Up to 300 users and up to five devices per user, with no minimum device requirement".

Who it's for

Small and midsize firms already on Microsoft 365 that want EDR without a second vendor.

Standout features

  • Automated remediation: it includes "endpoint detection and response with automatic attack disruption, automated investigation and remediation".
  • Next-gen antivirus: the plan lists "Next-generation antivirus protection" alongside vulnerability management.
  • Mobile and desktop: "Defender for Business protects Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android™ devices".

Integrations

It is sold on its own or inside Microsoft 365 Business Premium, per the Defender for Business page.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Defender for Business is "$3.00 user/month, paid yearly"; Business Premium is $22.00 user/month, paid yearly.

Switch if

Your company already runs Microsoft 365 and has fewer than 300 users.

Stay with crowdstrike if

You need a published 24/7 MDR service, which we found no Defender for Business pricing for.

Pros

  • $3 per user covers up to five devices each.
  • Free trial on the product page.
  • No minimum device count.

Cons

  • Servers need a separate add-on.
  • Capped at 300 users per the product page.
  • No device control or firewall management listed on the page.

#2 So

Sophos Endpoint

Best for ransomware rollback · 8.1 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Sophos ↗

Sophos Endpoint is next-gen antivirus with file rollback, adaptive attack defenses and an optional managed detection service.

Why we picked it

Sophos has the widest platform coverage in this list. Its product page says CryptoGuard "ransomware rollback combine in one lightweight agent that covers Windows, macOS, and Linux".

Who it's for

Teams whose top worry is ransomware and who buy through a reseller or MSP.

Standout features

  • Adaptive Attack Protection: "Sophos Endpoint instantly switches to a more aggressive protection to disrupt and contain the attack".
  • Mobile coverage: the page promises "Protection and visibility for endpoints, servers, and mobile devices".
  • Managed option: you can "Explore managed detection and response with Sophos MDR."

Integrations

"Synchronized Security™ allows your control points to respond as one to the threat." It links Sophos firewall, email and XDR products.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Sophos shows no public price; the how-to-buy page routes buyers to partners. A free trial is offered.

Switch if

You want automatic file rollback after ransomware and run a mix of Windows, macOS and Linux.

Stay with crowdstrike if

You want to buy online at a published per-device price.

Pros

  • Rollback restores encrypted files automatically.
  • Covers desktops, servers and mobile.
  • MDR available as an add-on service.

Cons

  • No readable price on the product pages.
  • Device control and firewall management not named on the page.
  • Buying usually runs through a partner.

#3 S1

SentinelOne Singularity

Best like-for-like EDR · 7.9 /10 (editorial score) · Visit SentinelOne ↗

SentinelOne Singularity is an AI-driven EPP and XDR platform with device control, firewall control and managed threat hunting.

Why we picked it

SentinelOne matches Falcon on all five protection checks. Every tier on its pricing page includes "Advanced EPP Controls (Device and Firewall Control, Remote Shell)".

Who it's for

Security teams that want a feature-for-feature Falcon replacement and buy through a partner.

Standout features

  • Autonomous response: every plan lists "Autonomous Prevention, Detection, and Response".
  • Managed hunting: Commercial adds "Managed Threat Hunting for proactive threat discovery".
  • AI triage: Enterprise adds an "Agentic AI SOC Analyst for automated triage".

Integrations

Identity, cloud workload and network discovery modules sit in the same console, per the pricing comparison.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Singularity Complete is "$179.99/yr. per endpoint", Commercial $229.99, and Enterprise is by quote.

Switch if

You want device control, firewall control and EDR in every tier, not only the top ones.

Stay with crowdstrike if

You want to pay monthly online without going through a reseller.

Pros

  • Device and firewall control on every tier.
  • Remote shell included.
  • Published annual prices for two tiers.

Cons

  • "All purchases will be made through an authorized third-party partner."
  • Complete keeps only "14 days of Data Retention".
  • No free trial shown, only a demo.

Integrations

The pricing page lists firewall management, patch management, DNS filtering and email security as modules.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, prices load in a cart we could not read; the pricing page notes "Save 20% for 3 years".

Cons

#6 Wa

Wazuh

Best free open source option · 7.7 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Wazuh ↗

Wazuh is an open-source XDR and SIEM platform you can self-host for free or run on Wazuh Cloud.

Why we picked it

Wazuh is the only free option here. Its homepage cites "no vendor lock-in, and no license cost" for the self-hosted platform.

Who it's for

Teams with in-house security engineers who want EDR-style detection plus a SIEM.

Standout features

  • XDR plus SIEM: "Unified XDR and SIEM protection for endpoints and cloud workloads."
  • Active response: "Wazuh automatically responds to threats to mitigate the potential impact on your infrastructure."
  • Threat intelligence: "Wazuh incorporates threat intelligence feeds to detect and respond to known threats."

Integrations

It pulls logs from third-party apps, cloud providers and SaaS tools over syslog or APIs, per the XDR page. See our SIEM tools list for more.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, self-hosting is free; Wazuh Cloud Small is "Starting at $571 /month" for up to 100 agents.

Switch if

You have engineers to run it and want endpoint detection without per-device license fees.

Stay with crowdstrike if

You need next-gen antivirus and a managed SOC rather than a platform you operate.

Pros

  • Free to self-host.
  • Cloud is "Compliant with PCI DSS and SOC2."
  • Cloud offers a free 14-day trial.

Cons

  • No next-gen antivirus or device control listed.
  • No 24/7 MDR service.
  • Self-hosting needs your own staff.

FAQ

FAQs about CrowdStrike alternatives for

What is the cheapest CrowdStrike alternative?

Wazuh is free to self-host. Among paid tools, Microsoft Defender for Business has the lowest published price at $3 per user a month, paid yearly.

Which alternative is closest to CrowdStrike Falcon?

SentinelOne Singularity matched Falcon on all five protection checks in our rubric. It adds device and firewall control on every tier but sells only through partners.

Is there a CrowdStrike alternative with an on-prem console?

Bitdefender GravityZone offers cloud or on-premise management, and Wazuh can be fully self-hosted. The others run from vendor cloud consoles.

Which alternatives include managed detection and response?

Sophos, ThreatDown and ESET all list a 24/7 MDR option, and SentinelOne sells managed threat hunting as an add-on. For log-based monitoring, see our SIEM tools list.
