OVERVIEW

Chroma is an open-source vector database for AI apps. Its docs call it "the open-source data infrastructure for AI."

Pinecone is a managed vector database. Its docs describe it as "a search engine for AI agents and applications."

FEATURE BY FEATURE

Search types

Chroma offers "Dense, sparse, and hybrid search." It also runs "Keyword and regex search over your data without embeddings."

Pinecone can serve full-text, semantic, sparse-vector and hybrid search "from one index."

Winner: Tie. Chroma adds regex, while Pinecone adds reranking.

Deployment

Chroma lets you "Run it locally, self-host, or use Chroma Cloud" for managed hosting. Pinecone is managed, with a BYOC option that runs "Pinecone in your cloud account" on Enterprise.

Winner: Chroma, for local and self-hosted use.

Embedding and reranking

With integrated embedding, "Pinecone generates the vectors from your text server-side." You can also filter by metadata, then rerank results "for relevance."

Winner: Pinecone.

Filtering

Chroma lets you "Filter results at query time by metadata conditions." Pinecone also applies metadata filters before reranking.

Winner: Tie.

Security and enterprise

Pinecone lists SAML 2.0 SSO on Standard and Enterprise, with SCIM on Enterprise. Chroma Enterprise lists "Single tenant clusters", dedicated support and SLAs, but no SSO on its pricing page.

Winner: Pinecone.

RAG tooling

Pinecone Assistant is "A managed service for RAG chat and agent applications grounded in your data". The Chroma pages we reviewed list no hosted RAG assistant.

Winner: Pinecone.

STRENGTHS

CHROMA PROS

  • Apache 2.0 open source
  • Runs locally, self-hosted or in Chroma Cloud
  • Keyword and regex search without embeddings

CHROMA CONS

  • SSO not listed on the pricing page
  • Team plan starts at $250 per month
  • No hosted RAG assistant listed in docs reviewed

PINECONE PROS

  • Integrated embedding and hosted reranking
  • SAML 2.0 SSO from the Standard plan
  • BYOC for Enterprise accounts

PINECONE CONS

  • Not open source; self-managed option is BYOC on Enterprise
  • Free Starter limited to AWS us-east-1
  • Enterprise needs $500 monthly minimum usage

FAQ

FAQs about Chroma vs Pinecone

Is Chroma better than Pinecone?

It depends on hosting. Chroma suits teams that want open source and self-hosting, while Pinecone suits teams that want a managed service with SSO and hosted embedding.

Is Chroma free?

Yes, Chroma is open source under Apache 2.0. Chroma Cloud Starter costs $0 plus usage and includes $5 in free credits.

Does Pinecone have a free tier?

Yes. Pinecone Starter is free with up to 2 GB of storage, 5 indexes and 2 users, limited to AWS us-east-1.

Can I self-host Pinecone?

Not in the open-source sense. Pinecone offers BYOC on Enterprise, which runs Pinecone inside your own cloud account.
