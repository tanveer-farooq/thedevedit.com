Cr No. 01Best for full EDR at published prices

CrowdStrike Falcon

 8.8/10

CrowdStrike Falcon is a cloud endpoint platform with published per-device prices from NGAV up to EDR and threat hunting.

Visit CrowdStrike Falcon

Why we picked it

Falcon is the only tool here to score full marks on protection coverage. Its pricing page lists NGAV, device control, host firewall, EDR, threat hunting and MDR across four tiers.

Who it's for

Small and mid-size teams that want EDR at a published price without a sales call.

Standout features

  • Device control on every plan: "visibility and precise control required to enable safe usage of removable media devices".
  • Host firewall from Pro: "centralized host firewall management, making it easy to manage and control host firewall policies."
  • Three desktop OSes: the platform "supports Windows, MacOS, and Linux operating systems."
  • Managed tier: Falcon Complete adds 24/7 MDR on top of Enterprise features.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Falcon Go is $7.99/device/month (100-device cap), Pro $14.99, Enterprise $19.99; Complete is contact sales. 15-day trial, no card.

Pros

  • Every tier except Complete shows a public price.
  • Free 15-day trial needs no credit card.
  • EDR and threat hunting arrive on Enterprise.

Cons

  • Falcon Go is capped at 100 devices.
  • We found no on-prem console on its pages.
  • Falcon Complete pricing is contact sales only.

FAQs about endpoint security software

What is endpoint security software?

Endpoint security software protects laptops, desktops and servers from malware and attacks. Most tools here combine next-gen antivirus with EDR, which records device activity so you can detect, investigate and respond to threats, and several add device control and host firewall management.

What is the difference between EDR and MDR?

EDR is the software that detects and responds to threats on endpoints; MDR adds a team of analysts who watch those alerts for you around the clock. Huntress includes a 24/7 SOC in its $8.99 price, while CrowdStrike sells MDR as Falcon Complete.

Is there free endpoint security software for business?

Yes: Wazuh is open source and free to self-host, and Wazuh Cloud starts at $571 a month for 100 agents. The other six tools offer free trials rather than free tiers, from 15 days on CrowdStrike to 30 days on Sophos.

Is Microsoft Defender enough for a small business?

For many Microsoft 365 shops, yes. Defender for Business costs $3 per user per month, paid yearly, and covers Windows, macOS and mobile. Teams that need Linux servers or advanced hunting should look at Defender for Endpoint or the $12 Defender Suite.

Which endpoint security tools offer an on-prem console?

Bitdefender GravityZone offers a choice of cloud or on-premise management, and Wazuh can be fully self-hosted. The other five tools on this list document cloud consoles only.
