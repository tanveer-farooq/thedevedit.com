Nb

NetBird

Best open source zero trust network

NetBird is an open-source zero trust network built on WireGuard that you can run in its cloud or on your own servers.

9.0/10 Visit NetBird

Why we picked it

NetBird takes full marks on entry access and admin controls. It is "distributed under a permissive BSD-3 license and can be self-hosted on your servers", and the pricing page lists IdP SSO, SCIM provisioning, audit and traffic event logs.

Who it's for

Teams replacing a legacy VPN that want the option to self-host the control plane.

Standout features

  • WireGuard mesh: it replaces legacy VPNs "with a peer-to-peer WireGuard®-based network".
  • Device posture checks: access goes only to devices that meet your security posture rules.
  • Traffic event logging: Business logs connection traffic events, and events stream to SIEM platforms.
  • Self-hosting: the BSD-3 licensed platform runs on your own servers.

Integrations

Identity providers include Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, Keycloak, Authentik and Zitadel, per NetBird's identity provider docs.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free is €0 for up to 5 users, Team €6/user/month, Business €12/user/month; Enterprise is "Contact us".

Pros

  • Free plan covers 5 users and 100 machines.
  • SSO and SCIM start on the €6 Team plan.
  • Self-hosting is available under a BSD-3 license.

Cons

  • We found no threat detection or IPS in its docs.
  • Posture checks and traffic logs need Business.
  • Prices are listed in EUR only.

Tailscale

Best managed WireGuard mesh

Tailscale is a managed WireGuard mesh VPN with per-user access rules, SOC 2 Type II certification and a free personal plan.

8.7/10 Visit Tailscale

Why we picked it

Tailscale scores full marks on entry access and admin controls. Its security page states SOC 2 Type II, and "All plans get access to Webhooks and Configuration audit logs!"

Who it's for

Teams that want encrypted device-to-device access without running a coordination server.

Standout features

  • ACLs: rules define "what users, groups, IP addresses, CIDRs, hosts, and tags can connect to each other".
  • End-to-end encryption: traffic between devices runs over WireGuard tunnels.
  • Network flow logs: "Premium and higher plans can now access network flow logs".
  • Audit logs on every plan: configuration audit logs and webhooks come with the free tier.

Integrations

Integrations include Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, CrowdStrike, Jamf, Intune and Splunk, per Tailscale's integrations page.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Personal is free, Standard $8/user/month, Premium $18/user/month; Enterprise is custom.

Pros

  • Personal plan is "Free forever" for up to 6 users.
  • SOC 2 Type II is stated on its security page.
  • Configuration audit logs ship on every plan.

Cons

  • We found no self-hosted control server in its docs.
  • Network flow logs need Premium at $18 a user.
  • We found no threat detection or IPS in its docs.

pfSense

Best firewall with deployment choice

pfSense is a firewall and VPN platform from Netgate that runs on its appliances, your own hardware or AWS and Azure.

8.2/10 Visit pfSense

Why we picked it

pfSense is the only tool here with full marks on deployment. Netgate sells it "as appliance, bare metal / virtual machine software, and cloud software", and Community Edition costs nothing.

Who it's for

Network teams that want one firewall across office hardware, VMs and public cloud.

Standout features

  • Turnkey appliances: Netgate sells ready-to-run hardware alongside software for third-party hardware.
  • Cloud images: you can "Select from AWS/Azure Marketplace" for a cloud firewall.
  • Free Community Edition: CE is listed at "No Charge".
  • TAC support tiers: paid subscriptions add vendor support to pfSense Plus.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, CE is free; pfSense Plus with TAC Lite is $129/yr per instance, with higher TAC tiers above.

Pros

  • Runs on appliances, bare metal, VMs, AWS and Azure.
  • Community Edition is free.
  • Per-instance prices are published, no sales call needed.

Cons

  • We found no SSO or SCIM in its docs.
  • We found no SOC 2 or ISO 27001 statement.
  • We found no device posture checks in its docs.

Twingate

Best VPN replacement for small teams

Twingate is a cloud zero trust access service that replaces a legacy VPN, with device posture checks and a free plan for five users.

8.0/10 Visit Twingate

Why we picked it

Twingate pairs a free Starter plan with a $5 Teams plan. Its pricing page lists SSO, IdP provisioning, admin audit logs and native device posture checks.

Who it's for

Small and mid-size teams retiring a VPN that want cheap per-user zero trust access.

Standout features

  • Device posture: Teams includes "Native device posture checks".
  • IdP provisioning: Business adds "User provisioning & management via IdP".
  • Admin audit logs: "Audit logs for changes to Admin Console."
  • DNS filtering: offered on Business and Enterprise as a paid add-on.

Integrations

Identity integrations include Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and Google Workspace, per Twingate's pricing page.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Starter is free for 5 users, Teams is $5/user/month, Business $10/user/month, Enterprise custom.

Pros

  • Free Starter plan covers up to 5 users.
  • Teams costs $5 per user a month.
  • Posture checks start on the Teams plan.

Cons

  • Teams SSO is limited to Google Workspace.
  • DNS filtering costs extra, even on Business.
  • We found no SOC 2 statement on its pricing page.

OPNsense

Best open source firewall with IPS

OPNsense is an open source firewall and routing platform with Suricata inline intrusion prevention, built-in VPNs and a €149 Business Edition.

7.6/10 Visit OPNsense

Why we picked it

OPNsense is free, self-installed and ships inline IPS. The Business Edition costs €149 a year per installation and adds an OVA image.

Who it's for

Teams that want a free, self-hosted firewall with intrusion prevention and are happy to run it themselves.

Standout features

  • Inline IPS: "advanced inline intrusion prevention using Suricata" blocks trojans and CNC bots.
  • Built-in VPN: "Integrated support for IPsec (including route based), OpenVPN", plus WireGuard.
  • NetFlow reporting: its analyzer "shows most active users, interfaces, ports, and applications."
  • Business extras: OVA image, GeoIP database and the OPNcentral plugin for central upgrades.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, OPNsense is free; Business Edition is €149/year for one installation.

Pros

  • Free open source edition with Suricata IPS.
  • Business Edition is €149 a year, published openly.
  • NetFlow reporting is built in.

Cons

  • Each Business license covers only one installation.
  • We found no SSO or audit log features in its docs.
  • We found no SOC 2 or ISO 27001 statement.

Zscaler

Best enterprise cloud security edge

Zscaler is a cloud security platform whose Private Access product replaces VPNs with inspected, one-to-one user-to-app connections.

6.8/10 Visit Zscaler

Why we picked it

Zscaler has the highest network protection score here. Its ZPA page covers access policies, VPN replacement, inline inspection and app segmentation.

Missing prices and no free tier pull it down.

Who it's for

Large companies that want to retire VPNs and inspect private app traffic from one cloud service.

Standout features

  • VPN replacement: "ZPA can replace legacy tools like VPNs and VDI in just hours."
  • One-to-one access: it "brokers direct, one-to-one connections between authorized users and specific apps."
  • Inline inspection: it inspects all traffic and content in a cloud native proxy architecture
  • Segmentation: user-to-app and app-to-app segmentation, with AI-generated segment and policy recommendations.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, the pricing page lists Essentials and Zscaler Platform bundles but no prices; you request a demo.

Pros

  • Users never reach the corporate network directly.
  • Inline inspection covers private app traffic.
  • AI suggests app segments and access policies.

Cons

  • No prices are published on its pricing page.
  • We found no free tier in its docs.
  • We found no device posture checks on fetched pages.

Tenable Nessus

Best network vulnerability scanning

Tenable Nessus is a vulnerability scanner with 319K+ plugins that audits network hosts for CVEs, misconfigurations and compliance gaps.

6.2/10 Visit Tenable Nessus

Why we picked it

Nessus is the one scanner in this list, and its prices are public on the Tenable buy page. It scores low on coverage because scanning is its only job.

Who it's for

Security teams that need to find vulnerable hosts and misconfigurations across their own network.

Standout features

  • Large plugin library: "117 K+" CVEs and "319 K+" plugins, with 100+ new plugins weekly.
  • Compliance audits: both editions include "Configuration, compliance, and security audits."
  • Runs anywhere: "You can deploy Nessus on a variety of platforms, including Raspberry Pi."
  • Expert extras: Expert adds web app scanning and external attack surface discovery.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Nessus Professional is $4,790 a year and Expert $6,790 a year; both offer free trials.

Pros

  • Prices are published, including multi-year discounts.
  • Unlimited vulnerability scanning on Professional.
  • Deploys on many platforms, even Raspberry Pi.

Cons

  • We found no free edition on its buy page.
  • Scanning only, with no firewall or access control.
  • Entry price is high for small teams.

FAQ

FAQs about network security tools

What are network security tools?

They are tools that control and inspect traffic on your network. This list covers zero trust access (NetBird, Tailscale, Twingate, Zscaler), firewalls (pfSense, OPNsense) and vulnerability scanning (Tenable Nessus).

Which network security tools are free?

NetBird, Tailscale, pfSense, Twingate and OPNsense all have a free option. NetBird and Twingate are free for up to 5 users, Tailscale Personal is free for non-commercial use, and pfSense CE and OPNsense are free open source firewalls.

What is the best open source firewall?

pfSense scored highest (8.2/10) for running on appliances, bare metal, VMs, AWS and Azure. OPNsense (7.6/10) is the pick if you want built-in Suricata inline intrusion prevention, with a €149 a year Business Edition.

Can zero trust access replace a VPN?

Yes. NetBird, Tailscale, Twingate and Zscaler all position themselves as VPN replacements. Zscaler says ZPA "can replace legacy tools like VPNs and VDI in just hours", while Twingate starts at $5 a user a month.

Does Tenable Nessus have a free version?

No. We found no free edition on Tenable's buy page, only free trials. Nessus Professional costs $4,790 a year and Expert $6,790 a year.
