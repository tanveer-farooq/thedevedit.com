The 7 best DevOps security tools, reviewed

Gl No. 01Best all-in-one pipeline scanning

GitLab

 9.3/10

GitLab is a DevSecOps platform that runs code, dependency, container and runtime scans inside its own CI/CD pipelines.

Visit GitLab

Why we picked it

GitLab is the only tool here that covers all six security checks in our rubric. Its feature comparison lists software composition analysis, IaC checks and attack simulation against running apps, and it runs on GitLab.com or Self-Managed.

Who it's for

Teams that want scanning, merge requests and CI/CD in one platform instead of stitching tools together.

Standout features

  • Findings where developers work: "Security findings appear directly in merge requests and IDEs", before code is merged.
  • Broad scan surface: it "scans your project's source code, dependencies, libraries, and container images."
  • Runtime testing: it runs "simulated attacks and fuzz testing against your deployed application".
  • Policy controls: security policies "Enforce organization-wide controls for security scans".

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free is $0, Premium is $29/user/month billed annually, and Ultimate is custom. Self-Managed uses the same tiers.

Pros

  • Covers all six security checks in our rubric.
  • Same tiers on GitLab.com and Self-Managed.
  • Free tier needs no credit card.

Cons

  • Enterprise-grade scanning is limited to the Ultimate tier.
  • Ultimate pricing is "Contact us" only.
  • We found no RBAC or SOC 2 details on its pricing pages.

JF No. 02Best for artifact and binary scanning

JFrog

 9.0/10

JFrog is an artifact platform that scans binaries, containers and code for vulnerabilities, secrets and IaC issues.

Visit JFrog

Why we picked it

JFrog covers five of our six security checks from the place where builds are stored. Its pricing page lists code and binary SCA, container scanning, and Advanced Security for secrets, IaC and SAST.

Who it's for

Teams that already store packages and images in Artifactory and want scanning at the artifact layer.

Standout features

  • Binary SCA: "Code & Binary SCA" is included from Enterprise X up.
  • Container scanning: "Container Scanning" ships with Enterprise X and above.
  • Advanced Security: adds "Secrets Scanning", IaC scanning and SAST code scanning.
  • Identity management: "OOTB identity management integrations using SAML and SCIM" on Enterprise X.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Enterprise X starts at $950/month on SaaS or $51,000/year self-managed; Pro lists $150/month, shown discounted to $50; Enterprise+ is custom.

Pros

  • Scans binaries; IDE plugins, CLI and FrogBot on every tier.
  • SaaS and self-managed prices are both published.
  • SAML and SCIM come with Enterprise X.

Cons

  • We found no free tier on its pricing page.
  • Secrets, IaC and SAST need Advanced Security.
  • Enterprise+ pricing is "Let's Talk" only.

Do No. 03Best for syncing secrets to CI/CD

Doppler

 7.9/10

Doppler is a secrets manager that syncs credentials to CI/CD and cloud platforms, with automated rotation and SOC 2 certification.

Visit Doppler

Why we picked it

Doppler pairs a free tier and a published Team price with strong admin controls. Its pricing page lists SAML SSO, role-based access and activity logs, and its security page states SOC 2 and ISO 27001.

Who it's for

Platform teams that want one source of truth for secrets across pipelines and environments.

Standout features

  • Pipeline sync: "Automatically sync your secrets and redeploy your infrastructure" when values change.
  • Rotation: "Automated rotation to mitigate leaks and respond to breaches".
  • Deployment choice: Enterprise runs as "Doppler On-prem or Cloud".
  • Activity logs: Team keeps "90 Days of activity logs"; Enterprise sets custom retention.

Integrations

Integrations include Vercel, Heroku and AWS Secrets Manager, per Doppler's security page.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Developer is free for 3 users, then $8/month per extra user; Team is $21/user/month; Enterprise is custom.

Pros

  • Free for up to three users.
  • SAML SSO and RBAC on the Team plan.
  • States both SOC 2 and ISO 27001.

Cons

  • Manages secrets only; no code or image scanning.
  • We found no pull request or IDE feedback in its docs.
  • On-prem deployment needs the Enterprise plan.

In No. 04Best open source secrets platform

Infisical

 7.9/10

Infisical is an open-source secrets platform with secret scanning that you can self-host or run in its cloud.

Visit Infisical

Why we picked it

Infisical takes full marks on deployment and entry access. It is MIT-licensed open source with a self-hosted option, the cloud Free plan covers five identities, and Pro is $20 per identity a month.

Who it's for

DevOps teams that want open-source secrets management and leak scanning under one tool.

Standout features

  • Secret scanning: `infisical scan` checks a Git repository's history or a directory for leaked secrets.
  • Pre-commit hook: a hook runs infisical scan to block secrets before commit, locally or in CI.
  • Dynamic secrets and rotation: Advanced adds dynamic secrets and full rotation, per its pricing page.
  • Secret syncs: even Free includes 10 secret syncs across unlimited projects.

Integrations

Okta, Entra ID, Google, AWS KMS, GCP KMS and AWS CloudHSM, per Infisical's pricing page.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free is $0 for 5 identities, Pro is $20/identity/mo billed annually ($23 monthly), Advanced $40, Enterprise custom.

Pros

  • MIT-licensed, with a self-hosted option.
  • SAML SSO and RBAC start on Pro.
  • Every plan but Enterprise has a published price.

Cons

  • Pro keeps audit logs for only 30 days.
  • Free has no secret rotation or dynamic secrets.
  • Secret scanning beyond 25 developers is a paid Enterprise add-on.

Docker Scout

 7.6/10

Docker Scout is Docker's image analysis tool that builds an SBOM and matches it against a continuously updated vulnerability database.

Visit Docker Scout

Why we picked it

Pricing clarity scores 10 because every Docker plan has a published price, from Personal at $0 to Business at $24. Admin scores 9: Business adds "Single Sign-On (SSO)" and "Image and Registry Access Management."

Who it's for

Teams already building and pushing Docker images who want vulnerability checks without adding another vendor.

Standout features

  • SBOM inventory: Scout "compiles an inventory of components, also known as a Software Bill of Materials (SBOM)".

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Personal is $0; Pro $9 annual or $11 monthly; Team $15 or $16; Business $24 per user/mo.

Cons

  • Scans container images only, not source code or secrets.

AI No. 07Best for AWS workload scanning

Amazon Inspector

 7.6/10

Amazon Inspector is an AWS vulnerability management service that continually scans EC2 instances, ECR container images and serverless functions.

Visit Amazon Inspector

Why we picked it

Security coverage scores 8 because code security covers "SAST, IaC, SCA" at $0.15 for each scan type, on top of workload scanning. Pricing clarity is full marks: every usage rate is published.

Who it's for

Teams running workloads on AWS who want scanning that discovers resources without scheduling scans.

Standout features

  • Automatic rescans: it rescans resources when packages change or a new CVE is published.
  • Risk score: adjusts NVD base scores to your compute environment, such as network reachability.
  • Org-wide rollout: it "can be activated for your entire organization with a single click".
  • Suppression rules: filters hide unwanted findings; reports export as CSV or JSON.

Integrations

Inspector publishes findings to Amazon EventBridge and AWS Security Hub CSPM, and runs from the AWS CLI and SDKs.

Pricing

FAQs about DevOps security tools

What are DevOps security tools?

DevOps security tools add security checks to the software delivery pipeline. They scan source code, dependencies, secrets, infrastructure code and container images, and report findings in CI/CD, pull requests or the IDE so teams fix issues before release.

Which DevOps security tool is best overall?

GitLab scored highest on our rubric at 9.3/10. It is the only tool here covering all six checks we score: SAST, dependency scanning, secrets, IaC, container images and DAST, inside its own CI/CD pipelines.

Is there a free DevOps security tool?

Yes. GitLab Free, Doppler (3 users), GitGuardian Starter (up to 25 developers), Infisical (5 identities) and Docker Personal (one Scout repo) all have free plans. Infisical is also MIT-licensed open source.

Which tools can be self-hosted?

GitLab, JFrog and Infisical publish self-hosted options. Doppler offers on-prem and GitGuardian offers self-hosting on their Enterprise plans. Docker Scout and Amazon Inspector are cloud or AWS-managed only.

Do I need a separate secrets manager if I scan for secrets?

Usually, yes. Scanners such as GitGuardian find leaked credentials, while managers such as Doppler and Infisical store, sync and rotate them. Infisical does both, with infisical scan alongside its secrets platform.
