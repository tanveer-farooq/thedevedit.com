THE REVIEW

Why it scores well

Falcon lists every protection layer our rubric checks, from antivirus and device control to EDR, threat hunting and managed response. Three of its four bundles show a public price, and the trial needs no card.

Who it's for

Small and mid-size teams on Windows, macOS and Linux that want EDR without a sales cycle, plus larger teams that want fully managed MDR.

Standout features

  • Device control on every bundle: USB, SD card and Thunderbolt policies across your fleet.
  • Host firewall from Pro: central firewall policy management starts at $14.99 per device.
  • EDR on Enterprise: continuous endpoint visibility with automatic detection and prioritizing of malicious activity.
  • Threat hunting on Enterprise: CrowdStrike experts hunt inside your Falcon tenant for signs of intrusion.
  • Mobile protection: detects malicious activity on Android and iOS devices.
  • Managed MDR tier: Falcon Complete adds 24/7 managed detection and response and a breach warranty.

Integrations

The CrowdStrike Marketplace says "All products – and vendors – that are purchasable via the Marketplace are screened, trusted, and validated to work with the CrowdStrike Falcon® platform." We did not count its connectors.

FAQ

FAQs about CrowdStrike Falcon

How much does CrowdStrike Falcon cost?

Falcon Go starts at $7.99 per device a month, Pro is $14.99 and Enterprise is $19.99 (as of Nov 2026). Falcon Complete, the managed tier, is priced through sales.

Is there a free version of CrowdStrike Falcon?

No, but there is a trial. The pricing page says "Our 15-day free trial provides access to CrowdStrike Falcon® Prevent (next-gen antivirus), CrowdStrike Falcon® Device Control, and Express Support."

Which Falcon bundle includes EDR?

Falcon Enterprise is the cheapest bundle that includes EDR and threat hunting. Go and Pro cover antivirus and device control, and Pro adds host firewall management.

Can I pay for CrowdStrike Falcon monthly?

Yes. The pricing FAQ says "Falcon Go, Pro, and Enterprise are available with monthly billing." For annual plans it adds "You can cancel within 30 days for a full refund."

What is Falcon Complete?

Falcon Complete is the managed tier, listed as "24/7 expert-led, AI-accelerated managed detection and response (MDR)" on the pricing page. It also carries the CrowdStrike Breach Prevention Warranty.
