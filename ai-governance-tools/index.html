Credo AI

Best overall

Credo AI is a governance platform that inventories every AI system, including agents, and maps them to ready-made policy packs.

9.6/10 Visit Credo AI

Why we picked it

Credo scores full marks on inventory, risk workflow, frameworks, monitoring and integrations. Its product page covers an "inventory of every AI system, including agents, models, apps, and shadow AI", plus approval gates and automated evidence.

Who it's for

Risk and compliance teams that need one register of internal and vendor AI with framework-ready policies.

Standout features

  • Auto-discovery: builds the inventory "with auto-discovery across your enterprise", alongside an AI Registry and Vendor Registry.
  • Policy packs: "Pre-built policy packs for EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, and SOC 2".
  • Approval gates: "Governance workflows with approval gates" plus policy inheritance and aggregate risk scoring.
  • Agentic risk library: an "Agentic risk assessment library with mapped controls" for agent use cases.

Integrations

Integrations include AWS, Azure, Azure AI Foundry, LangChain, CrewAI, ServiceNow, Jira and Slack, per the Credo AI product page.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Credo AI publishes no prices; you talk to an expert for a quote.

Pros

  • Real-time compliance monitoring and alerts on governed systems.
  • Automated evidence generation and audit trails for auditors.
  • Vendor AI sits in its own Vendor Registry.

Cons

  • No public pricing; every quote goes through sales.
  • SSO and hosting details aren't listed on the product page.
  • Integration names appear as logos, not documented connector pages.

OneTrust AI Governance

Best for runtime monitoring

OneTrust AI Governance is a governance module that monitors AI behavior at runtime on Amazon Bedrock and Microsoft Foundry.

9.4/10 Visit OneTrust AI Governance

Why we picked it

OneTrust earns full marks on inventory, risk workflow, monitoring and integrations. It can "Continuously monitor AI behavior across Amazon Bedrock and Microsoft Foundry with platform-specific runtime signals", which few rivals document.

Who it's for

Privacy and risk teams already on OneTrust that want AI reviews and runtime checks in one place.

Standout features

  • Broad inventory: "Discover and inventory AI systems, models, agents, datasets, vendors, projects, and use cases."
  • Approval workflows: "Configure intake, approval, attestation, and signoff workflows" for each AI use case.
  • Violation logging: "Detect and log policy violations in real time" from runtime signals.
  • Audit evidence: "Generate audit-ready evidence" from assessments, approvals, attestations, policies and telemetry.

Integrations

Integrations include Amazon Bedrock, Microsoft AI Foundry, Google Vertex, Jira and Palo Alto Networks, per the OneTrust AI Governance page.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, OneTrust lists no prices for AI Governance; you contact sales for a quote.

Pros

  • Covers homegrown and third-party AI with one risk evaluation.
  • Maps policies and controls for ongoing compliance.
  • Bedrock, Foundry and Vertex supported out of the box.

Cons

  • Names no framework beyond EU AI Act, NIST and ISO 42001.
  • No public pricing for the AI Governance module.
  • SSO and hosting details aren't listed on the page.

Saidot

Best risk and control library

Saidot is an AI governance platform built around a library of 260+ risks and 620+ controls that systems inherit.

9.4/10 Visit Saidot

Why we picked it

Saidot scores full marks on inventory, risk workflow, frameworks and integrations. Its library covers "the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF and 110+ other policies", and models and agents sync from Microsoft Foundry and Amazon Bedrock.

Who it's for

Governance teams that want ready-made risks and controls instead of writing their own from scratch.

Standout features

  • Saidot Library: "260+ risks, 620+ controls, 110+ policies and 170+ AI models and products."
  • MCP servers: "Three MCP servers let your own AI assistants register agents and draft risk assessments."
  • Inherited risk: "Systems inherit risks from the third-party models, datasets and products they link."
  • Tool-level classification: "Every tool an agent can reach is risk-classified, not just the model."

Integrations

Integrations include Microsoft Foundry, Azure, Amazon Bedrock, Slack and Jira, plus webhooks and the REST API for events.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, the Saidot pricing page shows no amounts; it says you can cancel before the next payment, with no long-term contract.

Pros

  • Named people review and approve what agents push.
  • Owners get risk-level alerts in Slack or Jira.
  • Plans can change at any time, no long-term contract.

Cons

  • Evidence is attached to controls by hand, then reused.
  • The pricing page lists no plan prices.
  • SSO details aren't listed on the pages we checked.

Microsoft Purview

Best for Microsoft 365 shops

Microsoft Purview is Microsoft's data security and compliance suite, which finds AI use, logs prompts and checks it against AI rules.

9.2/10 Visit Microsoft Purview

Why we picked it

Microsoft pitches Purview "as your front door to discover, secure, and apply compliance controls for AI usage across your enterprise". It is also the only tool here with published usage rates.

Who it's for

Security and compliance teams already on Microsoft 365 or Copilot that need AI controls inside the stack they run.

Standout features

  • AI app discovery: covers Copilot and third-party apps; others are "detected through browser activity".
  • DLP for AI sites: can "warn or block users from sharing sensitive information with third-party generative AI sites".
  • AI regulation templates: EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 23894, ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF in Compliance Manager.
  • Audit trail: "prompts and responses are captured in the unified audit log".

Integrations

Supported AI apps include Microsoft Foundry, Copilot Studio, ChatGPT Enterprise and Anthropic Claude (Enterprise), with connectors for "non-Microsoft services like Salesforce and Zoom".

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Purview comes with Microsoft 365 licences or pay-as-you-go; Audit Standard is "$15 per 1M records ingested".

Pros

  • AI assessments "provided free for six months with Copilot or Agent licenses".
  • Assessments export to Excel for auditors and regulators.
  • AI interactions can be monitored for each user in your tenant.

Cons

  • AI templates count toward your purchased premium licenses.
  • No approval workflow or risk tiering for AI systems documented.
  • No registry of models and use cases documented.

FAQ

FAQs about AI governance tools

What does an AI governance tool do?

It keeps a register of your AI systems and runs each one through risk reviews, approvals and audit evidence. All seven here document an inventory, a risk workflow and mapping to at least two of the EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001.

Which AI governance tool has public pricing?

Only Microsoft Purview publishes rates, such as Audit Standard at "$15 per 1M records ingested" as of Nov 2026. The other six send you to sales or a demo for a quote.

Do these tools help with EU AI Act compliance?

Yes, all seven map controls or templates to the EU AI Act, but none makes you compliant on its own. They track systems, assessments and evidence; your legal and risk teams still decide each system's risk class and obligations.

Can AI governance tools find shadow AI?

Some can: Holistic AI and Credo AI document automated discovery, and Purview detects third-party AI apps through browser activity. Trustible, Optro and Saidot rely on intake or platform sync instead, based on the pages we reviewed.

Is Microsoft Purview enough for AI governance?

It is enough if your AI use sits mainly in Microsoft 365 and Copilot, since it covers discovery, DLP, AI regulation templates and audit logs. We found no documented registry of models and use cases or approval workflow, so model-building teams may need a dedicated platform.
