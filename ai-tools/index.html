// HOW WE EVALUATED

Seven ranked tools, one rubric, every claim sourced.

On November 14, 2026 we reviewed each tool's official documentation, pricing page, help-center articles and trust or security page. We did not install, run or benchmark any tool, and no vendor saw this article before publication.

How tools were chosen. We started from the shortlist in TheDevEdit's AI keyword research for this topic, and each shortlisted tool passed the same checks: generally available, public docs and a published starting price, updated in 2026, and possible to roll out to a team through a business plan or self-hosting. The 7 with the highest editorial scores make this list. The other 3 shortlisted tools (GitHub Copilot, n8n and Claude Code) sit under Also considered with other tools we checked, several of which recur in current "top AI tools" results.

How they are ordered. By editorial score, highest first. Ties at one decimal are broken by the unrounded total, then by the admin-and-data score. Each score is the weighted sum of five criteria, each scored from documented facts:

CRITERION WEIGHT
Capability breadthJobs the vendor documents for the product, out of six: writing and chat, cited research, file and data analysis, coding, agents and automation, model hosting. 10 = five or more, 9 = four, 8 = three, 7 = two, 6 = one. 30%
Free and entry access10 = free plan for the core job and paid entry at or under $10/mo. 8 = free plan and paid entry around $20/mo. 6 = free access only as a trial or through another paid subscription. 4 = no free option. 20%
Pricing clarityStarts at 10. Minus 1 each for: a sales-only top tier, usage credits or metered charges on top of the plan price, promotional or region-only prices, a plan whose billing basis is not stated. 15%
Integrations6, plus 1 each for: named connectors to third-party business apps, Model Context Protocol (MCP) support, a developer extension point (plugins, SDK, API, custom agents or code steps), a catalogue the vendor says holds 100+ integrations or connectors. 15%
Admin and data controlsStarts at 10. Minus 1 for each element we did not find on any business plan: SSO, SCIM (documented for syncing users or groups from an identity provider), audit logs, no training on business data by default (a vendor statement that business data its AI features handle is not used for training), retention controls, a compliance certification on the vendor's trust, security or data-protection page. An element that cannot apply to a product is marked N/A and not deducted; no tool here needed that rule. 20%

These are editorial scores from documentation review, not test results. A missing element means we did not find it in the vendor's pages, not that the product lacks it. Breadth carries the most weight, which pulls single-job tools down: Cursor and Hugging Face cover one job each on our count and take places 6 and 7, and GitHub Copilot, n8n and Claude Code, also one job each, fall below the top 7. All five would rank higher on a coding-only, automation-only or model-hosting list, so read the "who it's for" line, not only the number.

ChatGPT

Best overall

ChatGPT scores highest on our rubric: with Gemini, it is one of two tools here with full marks for breadth, free access and admin controls, and it loses fewer points than Gemini on pricing clarity (8 vs 7). OpenAI's pricing page lists chat, deep research, data analysis, scheduled tasks and the Codex coding agent across its plans, and the Free and Go plans let you trial it across a team before you buy Business seats.

9.6/10 Visit ChatGPT
Show details: who it's for, pricing, limitations, score breakdownHide details
WHO IT'S FOR Teams that want one assistant for writing, research, data analysis and light coding, on a business plan with SSO.
STANDOUT FEATURE Codex is part of the ChatGPT subscription. The Free plan includes limited Codex use, Plus expands it, and ChatGPT Business includes "ChatGPT and Codex across desktop and mobile apps", so light coding tasks may not need a second tool.
PRICING As of November 2026, ChatGPT costs $0 (Free), $8/mo (Go), $20/mo (Plus) and from $100/mo (Pro, with $100, $200 and $500 tiers). ChatGPT Business is $20 per user per month billed annually for 2 or more users, or $25 billed monthly, and Enterprise is contact sales. Plus has no annual billing.
LIMITATIONS OpenAI's pricing page says the Go plan "may include ads." Audit logs through the Compliance API and OpenAI's ISO/IEC 27001 certificate cover Enterprise and Edu, not Business.

Business data controls, as documented: ChatGPT Business includes SAML SSO, MFA and "no training on your business data by default", and Enterprise lists SCIM and "Data retention and data residency controls" (OpenAI pricing docs).

Claude

Best for transparent team pricing

Anthropic publishes a price for every Claude plan, Enterprise included: $20 per seat per month, billed annually, with a 20-seat minimum and usage billed separately at API rates (as of November 2026). The $17 Pro plan includes Projects, Research, Claude Code and Claude in Chrome.

9.5/10 Visit Claude
Show details: who it's for, pricing, limitations, score breakdownHide details
WHO IT'S FOR Teams of 2 to 150 people that want SSO and no model training by default, which Claude Team includes from $20 per seat per month.
STANDOUT FEATURE One subscription covers chat, Artifacts, Projects and Claude Code, and the Team plan adds admin controls for remote and local connectors, so an admin decides which outside tools Claude can reach.
PRICING As of November 2026: Free ($0, limited usage), Pro ($17/mo billed annually or $20 monthly), Max ($100/mo), Ultra ($200/mo), Team Standard ($20/seat/mo annual or $25 monthly), Team Premium ($100/seat/mo annual or $125 monthly) and Enterprise ($20/seat/mo annual plus usage).
LIMITATIONS SCIM, audit logs, the Compliance API and custom data retention are Enterprise-only; Team doesn't include them. On Free, Pro, Max and Ultra, model training is listed as "Opt-out", so individual users have to turn it off themselves.

Anthropic says Claude "now lists over 950 MCP servers in the connectors directory" (July 28, 2026). Anthropic's Privacy Center lists ISO 27001:2022, ISO/IEC 42001:2023 and SOC 2 Type I and Type II, plus a HIPAA-ready configuration with a business associate agreement (BAA) available.

Gemini

Best for Google Workspace teams

Gemini has the lowest paid entry price on this list: Google AI Plus costs $4.99 per month (as of November 2026). Its free plan already includes Deep Research, Gemini Live, Canvas, and image generation and editing.

9.4/10 Visit Gemini
Show details: who it's for, pricing, limitations, score breakdownHide details
WHO IT'S FOR Companies on Google Workspace, where the Workspace pricing page lists the "Gemini AI assistant in Gmail" on Starter and "in Gmail, Docs, Meet, and more" on Standard.
STANDOUT FEATURE Gemini Enterprise Business edition, built for teams up to 300 seats with "No IT setup needed", includes a no-code Workflow Builder and connectors to tools such as Microsoft 365, HubSpot and Jira. Google states that "your data... isn't used to train Google models" (Gemini plans page).
PRICING As of November 2026, Google lists Free ($0), Google AI Plus ($4.99/mo), Google AI Pro ($19.99/mo) and Google AI Ultra (from $99.99/mo), and says you can extend usage limits by purchasing AI credits. Gemini Enterprise starts at $21 per seat per month (Business edition) or $30 (Standard and Plus), with no billing term stated; Workspace plan prices vary by region.
LIMITATIONS Google sells Gemini through three separate offers (Google AI plans, Google Workspace and Gemini Enterprise), so check which one your admin can manage before buying. The Business edition stops at 300 seats and lacks VPC Service Controls and customer-managed encryption keys, which are listed only for Standard and Plus.

On the admin side, Gemini Enterprise accepts any identity provider that supports OIDC or SAML 2.0, and Workspace admins can search Gemini log events and set chats to auto-delete after 90 days, about 18 months or about 3 years. Gemini Enterprise can also reach other data sources through a custom MCP server. Google's research notebook now sits under the Gemini brand: notebooklm.google redirects to Gemini Notebook, which the Gemini plans page lists in the free tier.

Perplexity

Best for cited research

Perplexity is an answer engine that puts "Citations in every answer", including on the free plan (Perplexity pricing). Pro adds Deep Research and lets you pick the model for each query.

9.2/10 Visit Perplexity
Show details: who it's for, pricing, limitations, score breakdownHide details
WHO IT'S FOR Analysts, product managers and sales teams who need answers they can check against the original sources.
STANDOUT FEATURE Connectors. The Connector for Slack summarizes Slack messages, combines them with web results and can post findings back to a connected channel. It is available on Pro, Max, Enterprise Pro and Enterprise Max.
PRICING As of November 2026, per Perplexity's help center: Pro $20/mo or $200/yr, Max $200/mo or $2,000/yr, Enterprise Pro $40 per seat per month ($400/yr) and Enterprise Max $325 per seat per month ($3,250/yr). The free plan is $0.
LIMITATIONS Plans mix subscriptions with credits (Max lists "10,000 monthly credits"), so heavy use is harder to forecast. Enterprise per-seat prices appear in the help center, not on the main pricing page.

Paid plans also include Computer, which Perplexity describes as "an independent digital worker that completes tasks and workflows for you". Beyond the built-in connectors, you can add custom remote connectors through MCP; in an organization, an admin has to allow it first.

For teams, Perplexity Enterprise lists SSO and SCIM, audit logs and automatic file deletion, and states: "We never train our LLMs on enterprise customer's data." Perplexity's security page says it is "SOC 2 Type 2 certified by independent auditors."

Microsoft 365 Copilot

Best for Microsoft 365 shops

Microsoft 365 Copilot works inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, OneNote and Teams. With the Premium (add-on) license, it grounds answers in your emails, files, meetings and calendars through Microsoft Graph, according to Microsoft's Copilot overview.

8.7/10 Visit Microsoft 365 Copilot
Show details: who it's for, pricing, limitations, score breakdownHide details
WHO IT'S FOR IT teams on Microsoft 365 that need existing sensitivity labels, retention policies and audit to cover AI prompts and responses.
STANDOUT FEATURE Enterprise data protection. Under it, Microsoft states that prompts, responses and data accessed through Microsoft Graph "aren't used to train foundation models", and that Copilot inherits sensitivity labels, applies retention policies and supports audit.
PRICING As of November 2026, Copilot Chat is "included at no additional cost with eligible Microsoft 365 plans". The Microsoft 365 Copilot Business add-on lists at $21 per user per month billed yearly ($18 promotional rate for existing Business customers' first year, July 1 to December 31, 2026) or $25.20 billed monthly. Bundles: Business Standard with Copilot at $23.50 and Business Premium with Copilot at $32 per user per month, billed yearly. Copilot Cowork, Copilot Autopilot and Work IQ APIs are billed separately through Copilot Credits.
LIMITATIONS Without the add-on, Copilot Chat can't use organizational data through Microsoft Graph; you have to upload files or work from open content in Outlook and Teams. Copilot Code requires the add-on plus usage-based billing and is in Microsoft's Frontier preview program.

Microsoft has renamed the Microsoft 365 Copilot app to Microsoft Copilot. Beyond Microsoft 365, Copilot connectors bring in data from "over 100 prebuilt connectors", including Salesforce, ServiceNow and Jira. The EU Data Boundary doesn't apply to web search queries, and Microsoft says Claude models from Anthropic are currently excluded from it, so EU-based admins should check both before enabling web grounding or model choice.

Cursor

Best for agent-first coding

Cursor is a desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux that describes itself as "a coding agent for building ambitious software". Its Agent "can complete complex coding tasks independently, run terminal commands, and edit code", and its $8 Plus plan costs less than GitHub Copilot Pro ($10/mo) or Claude Pro ($17/mo billed annually), which includes Claude Code (as of November 2026).

8.4/10 Visit Cursor
Show details: who it's for, pricing, limitations, score breakdownHide details
WHO IT'S FOR Developers and engineering teams willing to move to a new coding app to get an agent built into it.
STANDOUT FEATURE Plan Mode. Per Cursor's quickstart, it researches the codebase, asks clarifying questions, drafts an implementation plan and waits for approval before writing code.
PRICING As of November 2026, Cursor's monthly prices are Free ($0, limited AI usage), Plus ($8), Premium ($30), Super ($100), Ultra ($200), Teams (from $30 per user) and Enterprise (custom). Higher tiers are priced by usage multiples, for example Premium is "4x more usage compared to Plus." When included usage runs out, Cursor offers top-offs, and third-party models are billed separately by usage (Cursor pricing). The pricing page shows no annual prices.

Cursor's security page lists a SOC 2 Type II attestation, ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and ISO/IEC 42001:2023. With Privacy Mode on, Cursor says "your code is never used for training"; its help center says Privacy Mode "is enabled by default for all team members" and is on by default for Enterprise teams, while individual users can turn it on in settings. Its docs list GitLab, Azure DevOps, Bitbucket, Slack and Linear among the tools it works with, and you can add plugins, skills, MCP servers and rules from one place.

The Hugging Face Hub "hosts over 3M models, 1M datasets, and 2M AI apps (Spaces)", according to its documentation. The Hub is not a work assistant: Hugging Face runs a separate chat app, HuggingChat, but its paid plans are built around hosting and serving models.

PRICING Free account with free CPU Basic and ZeroGPU Spaces. PRO is $9/mo, Team is $20 per user per month, and Enterprise is $50 per user per month through sales. Storage, Spaces hardware and Inference Endpoints are billed separately.

For its Inference Providers API, Hugging Face states that it "does not store any user data for training purposes." We found no customer retention control in the pages we read, which is why its admin-and-data score is 9.

// HOW TO CHOOSE

Four questions, in this order.

1

Which suite does your company already pay for?

If you run on Microsoft 365, start with the Copilot Chat you may already have, then price the Copilot add-on. On Google Workspace, Gemini is already in Gmail from the Starter plan.

2

What job comes first?

For writing, analysis and research, compare ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, or pick Perplexity when every answer needs a citation; use Hugging Face for hosting models, and see n8n under Also considered for self-hosted AI workflows. For software, pick Cursor for an agent-first coding app, or start with the coding agent your ChatGPT or Claude plan already includes (Codex, or Claude Code from Pro).

3

What does your security team require?

If you need SCIM and audit logs, check the plan, not the product: for example, ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor and Perplexity list both on their Enterprise tiers, and Hugging Face lists audit logs from Team and SCIM on Enterprise. Microsoft 365 Copilot applies your existing Purview retention policies and supports audit.

4

What will 10 seats cost per month?

At list prices as of November 2026: ChatGPT Business $200, Claude Team Standard $200, Gemini Enterprise Business $210 (billing basis not stated), Microsoft 365 Copilot Business $210 on top of the Microsoft 365 plan itself ($180 at the promotional rate for existing Business customers, offered July 1 to December 31, 2026), Cursor Teams from $300 and Perplexity Enterprise Pro $400. The ChatGPT, Claude and Microsoft figures assume annual billing.

// FAQ

Questions people actually ask

What is the best free AI tool?

Among the general assistants, ChatGPT and Gemini are the two that score 10 on our free-and-entry criterion. ChatGPT Free allows unlimited text chats "subject to abuse guardrails", and Gemini's free plan includes Deep Research and Canvas. For coding, GitHub Copilot Free includes up to 2,000 inline completions a month.

Are custom GPTs still worth building in 2026?

OpenAI is moving custom GPTs to plugins. Its migration guide says new GPT creation will stop and that at retirement "custom GPTs stop running and leave the GPT directory." It gives no date (as of November 14, 2026), so build new internal assistants as plugins, which OpenAI's docs describe as a way to "extend ChatGPT and Codex."

Which AI tool is best for beginners?

Use a general assistant with a free plan first: ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. If your company already uses Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, the built-in option (Copilot Chat or Gemini) avoids a new account and keeps your company's data controls in place.

Can AI build and ship software without a developer?

The vendors describe agents that handle most of the loop: Claude Code, part of Claude plans from Pro, "plans the approach, writes the code across multiple files, and verifies it works," and Cursor's Agent "can complete complex coding tasks independently, run terminal commands, and edit code." Cursor's Plan Mode still waits for your approval before it writes code. Keep a developer reviewing changes before they reach production.

ChatGPT or Claude?

On our rubric they are close (9.6 vs 9.5), and both cover five of our six jobs. ChatGPT has the cheaper $8 Go plan, while Claude publishes its Enterprise price and includes Claude Code from the $17 Pro plan. Business plans are the same price: $20 per seat per month billed annually.
