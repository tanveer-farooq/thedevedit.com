CG

ChatGPT

Best overall

ChatGPT is OpenAI's AI assistant for writing, research, data analysis and coding, and the top scorer on our rubric at 9.6.

9.6/10 Visit ChatGPT

Why we picked it

With Gemini, it is one of two tools here with full marks for breadth, free access and admin controls, and it loses fewer points on pricing clarity (8 vs 7). Enterprise adds SCIM and "Data retention and data residency controls" to Business's admin tools.

Who it's for

Teams that want one assistant for most desk work and a path from free accounts to managed Business seats.

Standout features

  • Codex included: Business includes "ChatGPT and Codex across desktop and mobile apps", and Free has limited Codex access.
  • Deep research: the Free plan lists "Limited deep research", and Plus expands it.
  • Scheduled tasks: Go and Plus can run tasks on a schedule, per OpenAI's pricing page.

Integrations

Integrations include Gmail, Google Drive, Slack, SharePoint, Outlook, Microsoft Teams and Salesforce through ChatGPT's plugin library.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free is $0, Go $8/mo, Plus $20/mo and Pro from $100/mo; Business is $20/user/mo billed annually or $25 monthly; Enterprise is by sales.

Pros

  • Business lists "No training on your business data by default".
  • Business adds SAML SSO and MFA from two seats.
  • Plugins can include skills and MCP servers for in-house tools.

Cons

  • OpenAI's pricing page says Go "may include ads."
  • Audit logs and ISO/IEC 27001 cover Enterprise and Edu, not Business.
  • Plus has no annual billing, unlike Business.

Claude

Best for transparent team pricing

Claude is Anthropic's AI assistant for chat, research, files and coding, with full marks here for breadth, integrations and admin controls.

9.5/10 Visit Claude

Why we picked it

Anthropic publishes a price for every plan, including Enterprise at "US$20/seat/month, billed annually, 20 seat minimum". Its connectors directory, which Anthropic said in July 2026 "now lists over 950 MCP servers", helps it take full integration marks.

Who it's for

Teams of 2 to 150 people that want SSO and no model training by default, which Claude Team includes.

Standout features

  • Connector controls: Team adds "Admin controls for remote and local connectors", so admins decide what Claude can reach.
  • Task hand-off: Pro lets you "Hand off and schedule tasks" alongside Research and Claude Code.
  • Files and code on Free: the Free plan can "Search the web, create files, and run code".

Integrations

Integrations include Slack, Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, Linear and Microsoft 365 through Claude connectors.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free is $0, Pro $17/mo billed annually or $20 monthly, Max $100/mo and Team Standard $20/seat/mo annually or $25 monthly.

Pros

  • Anthropic's Privacy Center lists ISO 27001, ISO/IEC 42001 and SOC 2.
  • Enterprise offers a "HIPAA-ready configuration (BAA available)".
  • Connectors work across Claude, Claude Desktop, Claude Code and the API.

Cons

  • SCIM, audit logs and custom data retention are Enterprise-only.
  • Model training is "Opt-out" on Free, Pro, Max and Ultra.
  • Enterprise adds usage billed at API rates on top of seats.

Gemini

Best for Google Workspace teams

Gemini is Google's AI assistant across its own app, Google Workspace and Gemini Enterprise, with the cheapest paid plan on this list.

9.4/10 Visit Gemini

Google's research notebook now sits under the Gemini brand: notebooklm.google redirects to Gemini Notebook, which the Gemini plans page lists in the free tier.

Why we picked it

Breadth, free access and admin controls all score 10, a set only ChatGPT matches here. It scores 7 on pricing clarity, the lowest on this list: Google sells AI credits to extend limits, Workspace prices vary by region and Gemini Enterprise states no billing term.

Who it's for

Companies on Google Workspace, whose pricing page lists the "Gemini AI assistant in Gmail" from the Starter plan.

Standout features

  • Workflow Builder: Gemini Enterprise's Business edition, with "No IT setup needed", lets teams build custom agents without code.
  • Custom MCP server: Gemini Enterprise can connect your data sources through a custom Model Context Protocol server.
  • Admin logs: Workspace admins can search Gemini log events and set chats to auto-delete after 90, 540 or 1,080 days.

Integrations

Integrations include Microsoft 365, HubSpot and Jira from the Gemini plans page, plus SharePoint, OneDrive, Confluence and ServiceNow connectors.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Google lists Free, AI Plus at $4.99/mo, AI Pro at $19.99/mo and AI Ultra from $99.99/mo; Gemini Enterprise starts at $21/seat/mo.

Pros

  • Gemini Enterprise accepts any OIDC or SAML 2.0 identity provider.
  • Google says Enterprise data isn't "used to train Google models".
  • Google lists ISO 42001, BSI C5 and FedRAMP High for Gemini.

Cons

  • Three separate offers: Google AI plans, Workspace and Gemini Enterprise.
  • VPC Service Controls and CMEK are Standard and Plus only.
  • Business edition caps at 300 seats.

Perplexity

Best for cited research

Perplexity is an AI answer engine that puts citations in every answer, even on the free plan, and adds Deep Research on Pro.

9.2/10 Visit Perplexity

Why we picked it

It takes full marks for integrations and admin controls, and loses a breadth point because we found no coding feature on its pricing page. Perplexity Enterprise lists SSO, SCIM, audit logs and "automatic file deletion up to 1 day".

Who it's for

Analysts, product managers and sales teams who need answers they can check against the original sources.

Standout features

  • Slack connector: it summarizes Slack messages, mixes in web results and can post summaries to a channel.
  • Computer: Perplexity calls it "an independent digital worker that completes tasks and workflows for you".
  • Custom MCP connectors: you can add custom remote connectors through MCP; in an organization, an admin allows it first.

Integrations

Integrations include Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Notion, Linear and Salesforce through Computer's connectors.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free is $0; Pro is $20/mo or $200/yr, Max $200/mo, Enterprise Pro $40/seat/mo and Enterprise Max $325/seat/mo.

Pros

  • Perplexity states: "We never train our LLMs on enterprise customer's data."
  • Its security page says "SOC 2 Type 2 certified by independent auditors."
  • The Slack connector works on Pro, Max and both Enterprise tiers.

Cons

  • Plans mix subscriptions with credits; Max lists "10,000 monthly credits".
  • Enterprise per-seat prices sit in the help center, not the pricing page.
  • Computer is a paid-plan feature; some free accounts get limited trial turns.

Microsoft 365 Copilot

Best for Microsoft 365 shops

Microsoft 365 Copilot is Microsoft's AI assistant for Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams, sold as an add-on to Microsoft 365 plans.

8.7/10 Visit Microsoft 365 Copilot

Microsoft says "The Microsoft 365 Copilot app is now called Microsoft Copilot".

Why we picked it

It takes full marks for breadth and integrations: Microsoft offers "over 100 prebuilt connectors", and federated connectors use MCP. Entry access scores 6, the lowest here, because free use depends on another paid subscription.

Who it's for

IT teams already on Microsoft 365 that want AI inside the apps and policies their staff use today.

Standout features

  • Enterprise data protection: prompts, responses and Graph data "aren't used to train foundation models".
  • Labels, retention, audit: Copilot inherits sensitivity labels, applies retention policies and supports audit.
  • Copilot Search: looks across Microsoft 365 and connected systems, then gives a concise answer "with a citation".

Integrations

Integrations include Salesforce, ServiceNow, Jira, Confluence, Box, Dropbox, Google Drive and Workday through Copilot connectors.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Copilot Chat comes with eligible Microsoft 365 plans; the Copilot Business add-on is $21/user/mo billed yearly or $25.20 monthly.

Pros

  • Business Standard with Copilot bundles both for $23.50/user/mo yearly.
  • You can build custom connectors with the Agents Toolkit or Graph API.
  • Users sign in with Microsoft Entra ID work or school accounts.

Cons

  • Without the add-on, Copilot Chat isn't grounded in Microsoft Graph work data.
  • Cowork, Autopilot and Work IQ APIs bill separately in Copilot Credits.
  • We found no SCIM step documented for Copilot users.

Cursor

Best for agent-first coding

Cursor is a desktop coding app for macOS, Windows and Linux whose built-in agent plans changes, edits code and runs terminal commands.

8.4/10 Visit Cursor

Why we picked it

It is the highest-scoring coding tool we reviewed, with full marks for free access and admin controls. Its Plus plan undercuts GitHub Copilot Pro ($10/mo) and Claude Pro ($17/mo billed annually), but breadth, at one job, keeps it sixth.

Who it's for

Developers and engineering teams willing to move to a new coding app to get an agent built into it.

Standout features

  • Plan Mode: per Cursor's quickstart, it researches the codebase, drafts a plan and waits for your approval before building.
  • Privacy Mode default: Cursor says "For teams, Privacy Mode is enabled by default for all team members".
  • One extension hub: "Add plugins, skills, MCPs, and rules from one place", per Cursor's docs.

Integrations

Integrations include GitLab, Azure DevOps, Bitbucket, Slack and Linear from Cursor's docs.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Cursor's monthly prices are Free ($0), Plus ($8), Premium ($30) and Teams (from $30/user); Enterprise is custom, with no annual prices shown.

Pros

  • Security page lists SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 42001.
  • Teams adds SAML/OIDC SSO and custom chat data retention.
  • Premium gives "4x more usage compared to Plus" at a fixed price.

Cons

  • "Plus includes Grok models only", per the pricing FAQ.
  • SCIM and audit logs are Enterprise-only.
  • Third-party models are "billed separately by usage" at API list price.

Hugging Face

Best for open models

Hugging Face is a platform for finding and running open models, on a Hub that hosts over 3 million of them.

8.2/10 Visit Hugging Face

Hugging Face runs a separate chat app, HuggingChat, but its paid plans are built around hosting and serving models.

Why we picked it

It takes full marks for free and entry access, and loses one pricing-clarity point, for hardware, storage and endpoints billed on top. Breadth counts one job, model hosting, and we found no customer retention control, so admin and data scores 9.

Who it's for

ML and platform engineers who evaluate open models or need to serve one behind an API.

Standout features

  • Inference Endpoints: dedicated hardware billed by the hour, such as a $0.03/hour CPU on AWS or a $0.50/hour NVIDIA T4.
  • Inference Providers: the Hub docs say "a serverless API is provided by Inference Providers" for programmatic access.
  • ZeroGPU: Spaces demos get NVIDIA RTX Pro 6000 Blackwell GPUs "only when needed".

Integrations

Integrations include spaCy, Keras, fastai and Flair as Hub libraries, plus AWS, Azure and Google Cloud for Inference Endpoints.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, a free account includes CPU Basic and ZeroGPU Spaces; PRO is $9/mo, Team $20/user/mo and Enterprise $50/user/mo via sales.

Pros

  • Security docs say it is "SOC2 Type 2 certified" and GDPR compliant.
  • Team includes SSO (SAML and OIDC), audit logs and storage regions.
  • Hugging Face stores no Inference Providers data "for training purposes".

Cons

  • A T4 small Space is $0.40/hour, listed as $288/month if left running.
  • SCIM provisioning needs the Enterprise plan.
  • Non-developers will get more from a general assistant on this list.

FAQ

FAQs about AI tools

What is the best free AI tool?

Among the general assistants, ChatGPT and Gemini are the two that score 10 on our free-and-entry criterion. ChatGPT Free allows unlimited text chats "subject to abuse guardrails", and Gemini's free plan includes Deep Research and Canvas. For coding, GitHub Copilot Free includes up to 2,000 inline completions a month.

Are custom GPTs still worth building in 2026?

OpenAI is moving custom GPTs to plugins. Its migration guide says new GPT creation will stop and that at retirement "custom GPTs stop running and leave the GPT directory." It gives no date (as of November 14, 2026), so build new internal assistants as plugins, which OpenAI's docs describe as a way to "extend ChatGPT and Codex."

Which AI tool is best for beginners?

Use a general assistant with a free plan first: ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. If your company already uses Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, the built-in option (Copilot Chat or Gemini) avoids a new account and keeps your company's data controls in place.

Can AI build and ship software without a developer?

The vendors describe agents that handle most of the loop: Claude Code, part of Claude plans from Pro, "plans the approach, writes the code across multiple files, and verifies it works," and Cursor's Agent "can complete complex coding tasks independently, run terminal commands, and edit code." Keep a developer reviewing changes before they reach production.

ChatGPT or Claude?

On our rubric they are close (9.6 vs 9.5), both cover five of our six jobs, and their business plans cost the same $20 per seat per month billed annually. ChatGPT has the cheaper $8 Go plan, while Claude publishes its Enterprise price and includes Claude Code from the $17 Pro plan.
