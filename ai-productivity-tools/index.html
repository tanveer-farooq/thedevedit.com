Otter.ai

Best for meeting transcription

Otter.ai is an AI meeting transcriber with AI Chat on its free plan and an MCP server listed on every tier.

8.7/10 Visit Otter.ai

Why we picked it

Otter scores full marks on integrations and entry access. Its pricing page puts "AI Chat within and across meetings" on the free Basic plan and lists SCIM, activity logs and a HIPAA add-on higher up.

Who it's for

Individuals and sales or ops teams that record many meetings and want searchable transcripts.

Standout features

  • Free minutes: Basic includes "300 monthly transcription minutes" at no cost.
  • AI Chat: ask questions within one meeting or across all of them.
  • MCP server: the pricing page lists the "Otter MCP server" for AI assistants.
  • API and webhooks: Enterprise adds "Otter API & Webhooks" for custom pipelines.

Integrations

Integrations include Salesforce, HubSpot and Zapier, plus AI Chat with Gmail, Google Drive, Notion and Jira on Enterprise, per Otter's pricing page.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Basic is free, Pro is $8.33/user/month billed annually, Business $19.99/user/month billed annually, Enterprise by demo.

Pros

  • AI Chat works on the free plan.
  • Pro costs under $9 a user billed annually.
  • SCIM provisioning and activity logs for admins.

Cons

  • SSO needs Enterprise with a 100-user minimum.
  • HIPAA compliance is a paid add-on.
  • We found no no-training statement on its pricing page.

Fathom

Best free AI meeting notes

Fathom is an AI notetaker with a free-forever plan, instant call summaries, Ask Fathom answers and a public API with MCP.

8.6/10 Visit Fathom

Why we picked it

Fathom scores 9 on AI features, the highest in this group. Its pricing page lists "Instant AI call summaries" on the free plan, AI follow-up emails, and "Public API & MCP".

Who it's for

Individuals and small sales or customer teams who want AI call notes without paying first.

Standout features

  • Free forever: the free plan includes instant AI call summaries.
  • Ask Fathom: AI answers within a single call, or across team calls on higher plans.
  • AI follow-up emails: drafts the post-call email from the meeting.
  • HIPAA support: a "Standard BAA" is listed on Team and Business.

Integrations

Integrations include Asana, ChatGPT, Claude, HubSpot, Salesforce and Zapier, per Fathom's pricing page.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free costs nothing, Premium is $16/user/month billed annually, Team $15 and Business $25 billed annually (2-user minimum).

Pros

  • AI summaries are free forever.
  • Public API and MCP for custom workflows.
  • Team plan starts at $15 a user annually.

Cons

  • Team and Business need at least two users.
  • SSO and SCIM need Enterprise.
  • We found no audit log or no-training statement on its pricing page.

Notion

Best for AI inside team docs

Notion is a docs and wiki workspace whose Business plan adds AI meeting notes, Notion Agent and Enterprise Search across apps.

8.4/10 Visit Notion

Why we picked it

Notion scores high on integrations and admin controls. Its pricing page lists SSO, SCIM, an audit log and zero data retention with LLM providers on Enterprise.

Who it's for

Teams that already keep docs, wikis and projects in Notion and want AI over that content.

Standout features

  • AI Meeting Notes: "Automate your meeting notes and follow-ups, no bot needed" on Business.
  • Enterprise Search: searches Notion plus connected apps like Slack and Microsoft Teams.
  • Notion Agent: works with context from Notion, connected apps and the web.
  • No training by default: Notion AI "will not use your data to train" models unless you opt in.

Integrations

Integrations include Slack, Google Drive, Microsoft Teams, Jira, Box, OneDrive and Asana, per Notion's pricing page.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free is $0, Plus is $10/member/month, Business $20/member/month, Enterprise custom pricing.

Pros

  • Zero data retention with LLM providers on Enterprise.
  • SSO starts on the Business plan.
  • AI sits inside docs your team already uses.

Cons

  • Free and Plus get only a Notion AI trial.
  • SCIM and audit log need Enterprise.
  • We found no compliance certification on its pricing page.

Best for AI in project work

ClickUp is a project management workspace with an AI Notetaker and Brain² credits on every plan, including Free Forever.

Teams that want AI inside the tool where they already plan and track project work.

  • HIPAA with BAA: "HIPAA support available" on Enterprise and Max.

Integrations

Connected Search covers Google Drive, OneDrive, Gmail, Microsoft Teams and Slack on Enterprise, per ClickUp's pricing page.

Pricing

FAQ

FAQs about AI productivity tools

What are AI productivity tools?

AI productivity tools are apps that use AI to take meeting notes, plan your calendar, manage tasks or draft text. The seven here cover meeting notetakers (Otter.ai, Fathom), docs (Notion), calendars (Reclaim.ai), project work (ClickUp) and task planning (Todoist, Motion).

Which AI productivity tools are free?

Otter.ai, Notion, Fathom, Reclaim.ai, ClickUp and Todoist all list a free plan as of Nov 2026; Motion offers only a trial. Notion's free plan includes just a Notion AI trial, and ClickUp Free Forever comes with 500 AI credits.

What is the best free AI meeting assistant?

Fathom is the strongest free pick on our rubric, since its free-forever plan includes instant AI call summaries. Otter.ai's free Basic plan adds AI Chat with 300 monthly transcription minutes.

Which AI productivity tools offer SSO?

Notion and ClickUp include SSO on their Business plans. Otter.ai, Fathom and Reclaim.ai keep SSO on Enterprise, and Otter's Enterprise needs 100 users. We found no SSO on the Todoist or Motion pricing pages.
