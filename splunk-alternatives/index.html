# The 7 best Splunk alternatives for 2026

Splunk Enterprise Security covers SIEM, SOAR and UEBA, but every edition is sold by quote. We ranked 7 alternatives from vendor docs and pricing pages, on a rubric close to our SIEM tools list.

TheDevEdit Editorial Team, Editorial team · 12 min read · 7 alternatives reviewed · prices checked Nov 15, 2026

THE SHORT ANSWER

Datadog Cloud SIEM is the best Splunk alternative on our rubric (8.3/10). It publishes a per-event price, ships 800+ detection rules and keeps 12 months of security data searchable.

Pick Microsoft Sentinel for Microsoft shops or Wazuh for open source.

WHY TEAMS SWITCH

Why teams look for a Splunk alternative

  • Quote-only pricing: both Enterprise Security editions end in a quote request, starting with "Essentials Edition Human-led, AI-accelerated defense. Get a quote".
  • Features split by edition: SOAR and UEBA are ticked only in the Premier column of Splunk's pricing table.
  • Volume-based billing: Splunk asks buyers to "Choose from activity-based, workload, or ingest pricing", so cost tracks data volume or usage.
  • Cloud SOC consolidation: teams on Azure or Google Cloud can use a SIEM in that cloud, such as Microsoft Sentinel.

#1 Dd

Datadog Cloud SIEM

Best for published per-event pricing · 8.3 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Datadog ↗

Datadog Cloud SIEM is a SaaS SIEM that runs threat detection on Datadog logs, with a list price per million analyzed events.

Why we picked it

Datadog takes full marks on pricing clarity, with a public per-event SIEM rate. Its pricing page promises a "real-time detection engine and built-in threat intelligence" with 12 months of searchable security data.

Who it's for

Teams already on Datadog for logs or APM who want security detections on the same data.

Standout features

  • Detection rules: Datadog says to "Defend against attacks with 800+ Detection Rules" from its research team.
  • Entity analytics: logs can be queried to "detect suspicious user and entity activities and patterns".
  • OCSF mapping: a guided processor can "normalize logs from any source into OCSF".
  • Flex storage: offers "Flexible long-term retention up to 15 months without the need to rehydrate".

Integrations

Datadog lists the option to "Utilize 1,000+ integrations" across network, identity, endpoint and SaaS sources.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, the price list shows "Cloud SIEM Per 1M analyzed events, per month" at $5 billed annually, $6 monthly, $7.50 on demand.

Pros

  • List price per analyzed event is public.
  • Searchable security data covers 12 months.
  • Same platform as Datadog logs and APM.

Cons

  • We found no SOAR feature on the Cloud SIEM page.
  • No free tier; a free trial only.
  • SaaS only, with no self-managed option found.

Switch if

You already send logs to Datadog and want a SIEM with a public per-event price.

Stay with splunk if

You need SOAR in the same product or a self-managed, air-gapped deployment.

#2 Gl

Graylog

Best for published annual licenses · 8.0 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Graylog ↗

Graylog is a log management and SIEM platform with a free Open edition and paid editions at public starting prices.

Why we picked it

Graylog takes full marks on entry access and pricing clarity, and Open includes pipelines and data routing. Its pricing page lists "Graylog Security Starting at $18,000 /year" and a free Open edition with no volume limits.

Who it's for

Security teams that want a predictable annual SIEM license they can budget before talking to sales.

Standout features

  • Free Open edition: "Graylog Open Always FREE Source-available under SSPL."
  • Anomaly detection: Graylog Security adds "Machine-learning anomaly detection".
  • Threat context: it adds "Risk multipliers using adversary campaign intelligence" and asset context.
  • Case creation: "Automatic case creation with investigation evidence collection".

Integrations

Graylog Open collects logs over Syslog, CEF, GELF and Netflow, and offers "Full back-end access through REST API and MCP server".

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Open is free; Enterprise starts at $15,000/year, Security at $18,000/year, licensed from 10 GB/day.

Pros

  • Starting prices are public for both paid editions.
  • Free Open edition has no volume restrictions.
  • Self-managed or Graylog Cloud.

Cons

  • We found no SOAR or AI assistant documented.
  • Paid editions start at $15,000 a year.
  • No federated search found in the pages read.

Switch if

You want a fixed annual SIEM price and a free edition to start on.

Stay with splunk if

You need SOAR, federated search and air-gapped deployment in one product.

#3 Cs

CrowdStrike Falcon Next-Gen SIEM

Best for Falcon endpoint customers · 7.9 /10 (editorial score) · Visit CrowdStrike ↗

Falcon Next-Gen SIEM is CrowdStrike's cloud SIEM, adding log search, agentic SOAR and data pipelines to the Falcon platform.

Why we picked it

CrowdStrike scores full marks on detection and response. Its product page says it "unifies cross-domain security data, coordinated multi-agent investigations, and automated response", with federated search and its own data pipelines.

Who it's for

Security teams already running CrowdStrike Falcon on endpoints who want SIEM in the same console.

Standout features

  • Agentic SOAR: "Charlotte Agentic SOAR brings reasoning, automation, and human oversight together".
  • Behavior baselines: the SIEM "learns normal behavior for each agent and its owner", then flags deviations.
  • Falcon Onum pipelines: pipelines ingest, enrich and "route data in real time".
  • Index-free search: CrowdStrike claims "150x faster search at petabyte scale", its own figure.

Integrations

A separate edition can "Extend AI-native operations to third-party EDR, starting with Microsoft Defender." Federated search also reads Amazon S3 data.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, no SIEM price is published on the product page; it offers a free trial and demos.

Pros

  • SOAR, UEBA and threat intel on one platform.
  • Federated search across distributed data sources.
  • Built-in data pipelines through Falcon Onum.

Cons

  • No published price or billing unit.
  • SaaS only; we found no self-managed option.
  • Free trial only, no free tier.

Switch if

Your endpoints already run Falcon and you want SIEM, SOAR and pipelines from one vendor.

Stay with splunk if

You need an on-prem or air-gapped deployment, which Splunk Enterprise supports.

#4 Gs

Google Security Operations

Best for one year of retention included · 7.9 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Google ↗

Google Security Operations is a cloud SIEM and SOAR with Google threat intelligence and a year of retention in every package.

Why we picked it

Google scores full marks on detection, with Gemini, UEBA and Mandiant intelligence. Its pricing section "Includes one year of security telemetry retention at no additional cost."

Who it's for

SOC teams on Google Cloud, or any team that wants long retention bundled into the license.

Standout features

  • Parsers and SOAR: Standard gives "full access to our 700+ parsers and 300+ SOAR integrations".
  • Custom UEBA: "Use YARA-L to create rules for your own user and entity behavior analytics".
  • Threat intelligence: Enterprise Plus adds "Full access to Google Threat Intelligence (which includes Mandiant, VirusTotal, and Google threat intel)".
  • Gemini search: teams can "Leverage Gemini to search your data" in natural language.

Integrations

SOAR playbooks orchestrate EDR, identity management and network security tools, with 700+ parsers and 300+ SOAR integrations, per its product page.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, the three packages show "Contact sales for pricing", billed on ingestion.

Pros

  • One year of retention at no extra cost.
  • SOAR included from the Standard package.
  • Mandiant and VirusTotal intel on Enterprise Plus.

Cons

  • Every package is priced by sales.
  • Standard caps at 1,000 single-event rules.
  • Cloud only; no self-managed option found.

Switch if

You want a year of retention bundled in and Google threat intelligence on the same platform.

Stay with splunk if

You must run the SIEM on your own servers or in an air-gapped site.

#5 Ms

Microsoft Sentinel

Best for Microsoft and Azure shops · 7.9 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Microsoft ↗

Microsoft Sentinel is a cloud-native SIEM in the Defender portal, with an analytics tier and a low-cost data lake tier.

Why we picked it

Sentinel scores full marks on detection and response, with playbooks, behavior analytics and threat intelligence. Its data lake is built for "cost effective retention of large volumes of security data for up to 12 years".

Who it's for

Security teams already on Microsoft 365, Defender or Azure who want SIEM in that stack.

Standout features

  • Playbooks: response uses "playbooks, which are a collection of remediation actions", built with Azure Logic Apps.
  • Data tiers: the pricing page says data "can be ingested into either the analytics or data lake tier."
  • Commitment tiers: reserve daily capacity "from 100 GB to 50,000 GB" at a discount.
  • Free trial: the first 10 GB a day is free for 31 days, per Sentinel billing docs.

Integrations

Besides packaged connectors, "You can also use common event format, Syslog, or REST-API to connect your data sources".

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Pay-As-You-Go or commitment tiers, with "savings up to 52% over Pay-As-You-Go rates" on commitments.

Pros

  • Data lake keeps security data for up to 12 years.
  • 5 MB free ingestion daily per user for key logs.
  • Billed per GB, with a 31-day trial.

Cons

  • Per-GB prices did not load as text for us.
  • Azure portal support ends March 31, 2027.
  • Cloud only; no self-managed option.

Switch if

Your endpoints and identities already run on Microsoft security tools and you want one portal.

Stay with splunk if

Your data sits mostly outside Azure or must stay on your own servers.

Integrations

FAQ

FAQs about Splunk alternatives for

Is there an open-source alternative to Splunk?

Yes: Wazuh is open source with no license cost, and Graylog Open is free and source-available under SSPL. Both can run on your own servers, and both sell a hosted cloud version.

Which Splunk alternative has a public price?

Datadog, Wazuh Cloud and Graylog publish prices as of Nov 2026. Datadog lists $5 per million analyzed events billed annually; Graylog Security starts at $18,000 a year.

Which alternative keeps data the longest?

Microsoft Sentinel documents the longest retention we found. Its data lake holds security data for up to 12 years, and Google Security Operations includes one year at no extra cost.

Can I run a Splunk alternative on my own servers?

Yes: Wazuh and Graylog both document self-managed deployments. The cloud SIEMs on this list, including Datadog, CrowdStrike, Google, Sentinel and Rapid7, showed no self-managed option in the pages we read.
