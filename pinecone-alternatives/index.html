# The 7 best Pinecone alternatives for 2026

Pinecone is a managed vector database, but we found no self-hosted option and its paid tiers carry monthly minimums. We ranked 7 alternatives from vendor docs and pricing pages, on the same rubric as our vector databases list.

TheDevEdit Editorial Team, Editorial team · 12 min read · 7 alternatives reviewed · prices checked Nov 15, 2026

THE SHORT ANSWER

Zilliz Cloud is the best Pinecone alternative on our rubric (9.6/10). It matches Pinecone on admin controls and adds an exit path: the open-source Milvus engine runs on your own servers.

Pick Weaviate for built-in embeddings or Qdrant for air-gapped self-hosting.

WHY TEAMS SWITCH

Why teams look for a Pinecone alternative

  • No self-hosting: we found no self-hosted option in Pinecone's docs; BYOC runs in your own cloud account, priced by quote.
  • Monthly minimums: the Standard plan carries a "$50/month min. usage" charge.
  • Enterprise-only controls: audit logs, CMEK and private endpoints need Enterprise, at "$500/month min. usage".
  • Postgres teams: apps already on Postgres can keep vectors in one database with pgvector on Supabase.

#1 Zi

Zilliz Cloud (Milvus)

Best for open source with managed scale · 9.6 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Zilliz ↗

Zilliz Cloud is the managed service from the company behind Milvus, an open-source vector database you can also run yourself.

Zilliz says its cloud is "Built by the creators of Milvus."

Why we picked it

It takes full marks on deployment and admin controls. You can "Run Milvus in your own environment.", use BYOC, or pick managed Zilliz Cloud, and its pricing page lists SSO, RBAC, audit logs, CMEK and SOC 2.

Who it's for

Teams that want a managed vector database today and an open-source exit path later.

Standout features

  • Hybrid search: it offers "hybrid retrieval, filtering, and reranking" in one query layer.
  • Multi-tenancy: "Unlimited namespaces with hybrid vector, full-text, and JSON search" for multi-tenant apps.
  • BYOC: with BYOC, "Your data stays in your VPC."
  • Self-hosting: the open-source Milvus engine runs on your own servers.

Integrations

You can subscribe through the AWS, GCP and Azure marketplaces, per the Zilliz pricing page.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free is $0 with 5 GB storage, Standard starts at $0/month serverless or $126/month dedicated, Enterprise from $197/month, and Business Critical is by demo.

Pros

  • Enterprise lists "Audit logs, SSO (SAML 2.0 based), granular RBAC".
  • Its pricing page lists "Customer managed encryption keys (CMEK)".
  • "SOC 2 Type II and ISO/IEC 27001 compliant", per its pricing page.

Cons

  • Free allows up to 5 collections.
  • Business Critical has no published price.
  • We found no built-in embedding on the fetched pages.

Switch if

You want Pinecone-level admin controls plus the option to self-host the same engine later.

Stay with pinecone if

You want hosted reranking and integrated embedding documented on one fully managed platform.

#2 We

Weaviate

Best open source with built-in embeddings · 9.5 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Weaviate ↗

Weaviate is an open-source vector database that builds embeddings for you and runs in its cloud, yours, or on your servers.

Why we picked it

Weaviate takes full marks on search, deployment and entry access. It runs as Weaviate Cloud or self-managed, offers "built-in vector generation", and has a free cluster plus Flex from $45 a month.

Who it's for

Developers who want open-source vector search without building a separate embedding pipeline.

Standout features

  • Hybrid search: it fuses vector and BM25F keyword results into one ranked list.
  • MMR reranking: Maximal Marginal Relevance reranks results to balance relevance with diversity.
  • Multi-tenancy: "Each tenant is stored on a separate shard", invisible to other tenants.
  • Built-in vectorizers: text and images become vectors with "no external embedding pipeline required."

Integrations

Model integrations include OpenAI, Anthropic, Cohere, Google, AWS, Mistral, Hugging Face and Ollama, per Weaviate's model provider docs.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free is $0 for one cluster, Flex starts at $45/mo pay as you go, and Premium is by sales.

Pros

  • RBAC is included on every plan, Free too.
  • Self-host it or use Weaviate Cloud.
  • Weaviate Cloud is SOC 2 audited.

Cons

  • We found no audit logs in its docs.
  • SSO needs Premium; customer keys need Premium dedicated.
  • Flex carries a $45 monthly minimum.

Switch if

You want open source and built-in vectorizers, with RBAC on every plan, Free included.

Stay with pinecone if

You need audit logs, which we found in Pinecone's docs but not Weaviate's.

#3 Qd

Qdrant

Best for self-hosting and hybrid cloud · 9.0 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Qdrant ↗

Qdrant is a vector database you can run in its cloud, on your own Kubernetes, or fully air-gapped on your own servers.

Why we picked it

Qdrant scores full marks on search and deployment. Its Hybrid Cloud lets you "bring your own Kubernetes", Private Cloud supports air-gapped setups, and its query API fuses dense and sparse search with re-scoring.

Who it's for

Platform teams that must keep vector data on their own infrastructure or Kubernetes clusters.

Standout features

  • Hybrid queries: you can fuse any combination of queries, then re-score candidates with a richer vector.
  • Filtered search: filters apply during the HNSW search phase, not only afterwards.
  • Tiered multitenancy: tenants share collections by payload field; large ones get dedicated shards.
  • Cloud inference: free cloud inference with selected models creates embeddings inside Qdrant Cloud.

Integrations

Integrations include LangChain, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, Haystack, CrewAI, Mastra, DSPy and Mem0, per Qdrant's frameworks docs.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free is a 1GB single-node cluster, Standard is usage-based and billed hourly, and Premium needs a minimum spend.

Pros

  • Cloud, Hybrid Cloud and air-gapped Private Cloud options.
  • Granular RBAC for Qdrant Cloud resources.
  • Homepage states "SOC2 & HIPAA compliant".

Cons

  • Standard shows no published dollar price on the pricing page.
  • SSO and private VPC links need Premium.
  • We found no audit logs in its docs.

Switch if

You must keep vectors on your own Kubernetes or in an air-gapped setup.

Stay with pinecone if

You want a published entry price, like Pinecone's $20/month flat Builder plan.

#4 tp

turbopuffer

Best for large search on object storage · 8.7 /10 (editorial score) · Visit turbopuffer ↗

turbopuffer is an object-storage native search engine that pairs vector search with BM25 full-text search and server-side rank fusion.

turbopuffer says it uses "only object storage for state and NVMe SSD with memory cache for compute".

Why we picked it

Its docs cover hybrid search, with "server-side rank fusion via RRF" and native reranking. Pricing clarity scores 9 because every tier has a published price, enterprise included.

Who it's for

Teams with large or fast-growing indexes that want search costs tied to object storage, not memory.

Standout features

  • Native reranking: set `rerank_by` on the multi-query to score and reorder combined candidates.
  • Filtered search: filters run "against an inverted index" alongside vector and full-text queries.
  • Built-in embedding: the quickstart says "turbopuffer embeds your text on writes and queries".
  • BYOC: deploy into your own AWS, GCP, or Azure account with Standard or Managed BYOC.

Integrations

Optional client-side embedding models include Cohere, Google Gemini and Voyage AI, shown in the turbopuffer quickstart, plus SDKs for Python, TypeScript, Go, Java, Ruby, Rust and C#.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, launch has a $16/mo minimum usage; scale has a $256/mo minimum; enterprise starts at $4,096/mo with a 35% usage premium.

Pros

  • SOC 2 report and GDPR-ready DPA on every plan.
  • SSO and audit logs start on the scale plan.
  • Per-namespace CMEK and private networking on enterprise.

Cons

  • We found no free tier on its pricing page.
  • Unused minimum amounts don't roll over to next month.
  • We found no RBAC in its docs or pricing page.

Switch if

Your index is large and you want storage costs tied to object storage, not memory.

Stay with pinecone if

You want a free tier to start; we found none at turbopuffer.

#5 Su

Supabase

Best for vectors inside Postgres · 8.2 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Supabase ↗

Supabase is a Postgres platform that adds vector search through pgvector, with hybrid keyword search and row-level security on results.

Supabase calls it "an open source toolkit for developing AI applications using Postgres and pgvector."

Why we picked it

Entry access scores full marks: a Free plan and Pro from $25/mo. Its docs show hybrid search with `tsvector` and `pgvector` in plain Postgres.

Who it's for

Teams already on Postgres that want embeddings next to their app data, auth and permissions.

Standout features

  • Permission-aware search: row-level security can restrict which documents a vector similarity search returns.
  • Embeddings in Edge Functions: generate embeddings using open source models directly in Edge Functions.
  • Self-hosting: run it yourself with the Docker setup or the Supabase CLI.

Integrations

Integrations include OpenAI, Hugging Face, LangChain and LlamaIndex, listed in the Supabase AI and vectors docs.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free is $0, Pro is from $25/mo, Team is from $599/mo and Enterprise is custom.

Pros

  • Team adds SOC 2, ISO 27001 and listed dashboard SSO.
  • Platform audit logs are included on Team and Enterprise.
  • Vectors, auth and app tables share one Postgres database.

Cons

  • Free projects pause after one week of inactivity.
  • We found no customer-managed keys in its pricing page.
  • We found no tenant partitions or reranking in its AI docs.

Switch if

Your app already runs on Postgres and you want vectors beside auth and app tables.

Stay with pinecone if

You need tenant namespaces and hosted reranking, which we found no Supabase docs for.

#6 Up

Upstash Vector

Best for serverless pay per request · 7.5 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Upstash Vector ↗

Upstash Vector is a serverless vector database billed per request, with hybrid indexes, namespaces and hosted embedding models.

Why we picked it

Search scores 9 because hybrid indexes "manage a dense and a sparse index component for you". Entry access is full marks: a free plan plus pay as you go at $0.4 per 100K requests.

Who it's for

Developers on serverless apps who want vector search priced by request, not by cluster.

Standout features

  • Hybrid indexes: one index holds dense and sparse components, so keyword and semantic search share it.
  • Hosted embedding: Upstash "provides some hosted models" so you can upsert and query raw text.
  • Namespaces: you can partition a single index into multiple isolated namespaces.
  • SQL-like filters: metadata filters use a syntax that resembles SQL.

Integrations

Integrations include LangChain and LlamaIndex, both documented in Upstash's own docs.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free is $0 with 10K daily queries and 1 GB; pay as you go is $0.4 per 100K requests; Fixed is $60/mo; Pro is "Contact Us."

Pros

  • Free plan allows 100 namespaces and 1 GB of data.
  • Fixed plan adds a 99.9% uptime SLA.
  • Hosted models remove a separate embedding pipeline.

Cons

  • We found no server-side reranking in its docs.
  • Managed only; we found no self-hosted or BYOC option.
  • We found no SSO, RBAC or SOC 2 on fetched pages.

Switch if

Your traffic is spiky and you would rather pay per request than a monthly minimum.

Stay with pinecone if

You need SSO, RBAC or SOC 2, which we found on Pinecone's pricing page only.

#7 Ch

Chroma

Best for local-first prototyping · 7.1 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Chroma ↗

Chroma is an Apache 2.0 vector database you can run locally, self-host or use as the managed Chroma Cloud.

Why we picked it

Chroma says you can "run it locally, self-host, or use Chroma Cloud", which suits prototypes that later move to production. Chroma Cloud also adds dense plus sparse hybrid search with RRF.

Who it's for

Developers prototyping RAG on a laptop who want the same API in a managed cloud later.

Standout features

  • Open source: "Chroma is licensed under Apache 2.0".
  • Hybrid RRF: Cloud search combines dense semantic and sparse keyword embeddings with rank fusion.
  • Filtered ranking: each RRF component "operates on the documents that pass the filter".
  • SOC 2 cloud: Chroma Cloud is SOC 2 Type II certified.

Integrations

Integrations include OpenAI, Cohere, Hugging Face, Ollama, LangChain, LlamaIndex and Haystack, per Chroma's integrations docs.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Starter is $0/mo plus usage with $5 free credits; Team is $250/mo plus usage with $100 credits; Enterprise is custom.

Pros

  • Usage rates are published: $2.50 per GiB written.
  • Storage costs $0.33 per GiB per month.
  • Same database runs locally, self-hosted or in Cloud.

Cons

  • Team plan jumps to $250/mo plus usage.
  • We found no SSO, RBAC or audit logs in its docs.
  • We found no tenant partitions or server-side reranking documented.

Switch if

You prototype locally and want the same Apache 2.0 database in production later.

Stay with pinecone if

You need SSO, RBAC and audit logs, which we found no Chroma docs for.

FAQ

FAQs about Pinecone alternatives for

Is there an open-source alternative to Pinecone?

Yes: Milvus, Weaviate, Qdrant and Chroma are open source and document self-hosting. Zilliz runs Milvus as a managed cloud, so you can start managed and move to your own servers later.

Which Pinecone alternative is cheapest to start?

Zilliz Cloud, Weaviate, Qdrant, Supabase, Upstash Vector and Chroma all list a free tier as of Nov 2026. Upstash Vector pay as you go bills $0.4 per 100K requests.

Can I self-host a Pinecone alternative?

Yes: Milvus, Weaviate, Qdrant, Supabase and Chroma document self-hosting. Qdrant also offers air-gapped deployments through its Private Cloud.

Which alternative is closest to Pinecone's admin controls?

Zilliz Cloud lists SSO, RBAC, audit logs, CMEK, private endpoints and SOC 2 on its pricing page. Pinecone lists the same set, with several only on its Enterprise plan.
