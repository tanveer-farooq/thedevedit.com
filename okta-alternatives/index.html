# The 7 best Okta alternatives for 2026

Okta sells single sign-on, MFA and lifecycle tools as annual suites. We ranked 7 alternatives from vendor docs and pricing pages, scoring access features, deployment, entry price, pricing clarity and integrations.

TheDevEdit Editorial Team, Editorial team · 12 min read · 7 alternatives reviewed · prices checked Nov 15, 2026

THE SHORT ANSWER

Microsoft Entra ID is the best Okta alternative for most teams on our rubric (9.4/10). It has a free tier with MFA and unlimited SSO, P1 at $7 per user a month, and an app gallery of thousands of apps.

Pick OneLogin for low per-user prices or authentik for self-hosting.

WHY TEAMS SWITCH

Why teams look for an Okta alternative

  • Contract minimum: Okta lists a "$1,500 annual contract minimum required for Okta Workforce Identity", and all suites bill annually.
  • Quote-only upper tiers: for Professional and Enterprise, Okta says "we recommend contacting our sales team for a custom quote".
  • Customer identity cost: Okta Customer Identity starts with a "required Enterprise base product at $3,000 per month, billed annually".
  • Already paying for a suite: "Microsoft 365 E3 includes Microsoft Entra ID P1", so some teams already own an identity provider.

#1 En

Microsoft Entra ID

Best for Microsoft 365 shops · 9.4 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Microsoft Entra ID ↗

Microsoft Entra ID is Microsoft's cloud identity service, with a free tier and per-user plans that undercut Okta's entry suite.

Why we picked it

Entra ID scores full marks on access features, entry access and integrations. Its free tier offers "MFA, unlimited SSO, and password self-service".

Who it's for

Teams on Microsoft 365 or Azure that want one identity provider for staff apps.

Standout features

  • App gallery: the gallery lists "thousands of applications that make it easy to deploy and configure single sign-on (SSO) and automated user provisioning".
  • Risk-based access: P2 adds "Risk-based Conditional Access".
  • Bundled licensing: P1 comes with Microsoft 365 E3, and P2 with E5.

Integrations

SSO and provisioning connectors come from the Entra app gallery.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Entra ID Free costs $0, P1 is $7.00 user/month, paid yearly, P2 is $10 and Entra Suite is $12.

Pros

  • P1 costs about the same as Okta Starter.
  • A free tier covers MFA and SSO.
  • P1 may already be in your Microsoft 365 E3 plan.

Cons

  • Plans are listed as paid yearly.
  • We found no self-hosted option.
  • Risk-based access needs P2.

Switch if

You already license Microsoft 365 E3 or E5 and pay for Okta on top.

Stay with okta if

Your apps and staff sit mostly outside the Microsoft ecosystem.

#2 On

OneLogin

Best for low per-user price · 9.0 /10 (editorial score) · Visit OneLogin ↗

OneLogin is a cloud SSO and MFA suite with four published plans, three of them priced per user.

Why we picked it

OneLogin matches Entra on features and integrations at lower prices. Its app catalog lists "Thousands of Prebuilt App Connectors".

Who it's for

Small and midsize IT teams replacing Okta Starter on a tight budget.

Standout features

  • Adaptive login: Business adds "SmartFactor Adaptive Authentication".
  • HR sync: Business plans include "HR Integrations (Workday, UKG, Namely, BambooHR)".
  • Directory sync: every plan syncs users with Active Directory.

Integrations

Apps connect through prebuilt connectors plus SAML and OIDC.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Basic is $3 /user/month with limited user management, Essentials $6, Business $10; Enterprise is "Call for Pricing".

Pros

  • Basic costs half of Okta Starter.
  • SSO and MFA come in every plan.
  • Prices are listed per user, not per suite.

Cons

  • No free plan, only a free trial.
  • Adaptive login and RBAC need Business.
  • Basic limits security policies to 5.

Switch if

You want Okta-style cloud SSO and MFA at a lower per-user price.

Stay with okta if

You rely on Okta Workflows automation for onboarding.

#3 Ak

authentik

Best for self-hosted workforce SSO · 9.0 /10 (editorial score) · Visit authentik ↗

authentik is an open-source identity provider you run on your own servers, with a paid Enterprise tier on top.

Why we picked it

authentik covers every access feature in our rubric at the lowest paid workforce price here. The open-source edition "Supports OIDC , SAML , LDAP , SCIM , RADIUS , Kerberos , and Proxy".

Who it's for

Teams that must keep identity data on their own infrastructure.

Standout features

  • Remote access: the open-source plan includes "Web-based RDP/SSH access".
  • Open core promise: authentik says "we will not move any features from the open source version to the enterprise version".
  • Legacy protocols: LDAP and RADIUS cover older apps and network gear.

Integrations

Setup guides for third-party apps sit in the authentik integrations catalog.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, open source is free, Enterprise is $5 /user/month for workforce accounts, billed annually; Enterprise Plus starts at $20k a year.

Pros

  • Workforce seats cost $5 a month.
  • Agent and service accounts are included.
  • Customer accounts cost $0.02 each.

Cons

  • "We do not currently provide a hosted version of authentik".
  • The free edition lists no support.
  • Enterprise bills annually.

Switch if

You want to self-host identity and cut per-user cost below Okta Starter.

Stay with okta if

You don't want to run and patch an identity server yourself.

#4 Fa

FusionAuth

Best for customer login in your apps · 8.9 /10 (editorial score) · Visit FusionAuth ↗

FusionAuth is a developer-focused auth server for app logins that you can self-host free or rent as a hosted plan.

Why we picked it

FusionAuth is the cheapest route here off Okta Customer Identity. "FusionAuth Community is free and unlimited", and hosted plans start well below $3,000 a month.

Who it's for

Developers adding login, registration and MFA to their own product.

Standout features

  • Free self-hosting: "Self-host FusionAuth on your own infrastructure for free".
  • Paid add-ons: "Starter adds Premium features including advanced MFA and application theming".
  • SCIM and WebAuthn: both appear in the plan comparison as premium features.

Integrations

FusionAuth lists quickstarts and SDKs for React, Angular, Python, Java, Go and other stacks.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Community is free, Starter starts at $162 / mo and Essentials at $2,970 a month.

Pros

  • Self-hosted Community has no fee.
  • Hosted Starter costs a fraction of Okta's customer base fee.
  • One product runs in cloud or on your servers.

Cons

  • Built for customer login more than staff SSO.
  • Advanced MFA needs a paid plan.
  • We found no conditional access in its pricing docs.

Switch if

You pay for Okta Customer Identity mainly to log users into your own app.

Stay with okta if

You need one vendor for both workforce and customer identity.

#5 Zi

Zitadel

Best for multi-tenant B2B apps · 8.8 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Zitadel ↗

Zitadel is an open-source identity platform with a hosted cloud, built around organizations for B2B products.

Why we picked it

Zitadel offers both cloud and self-hosting, plus a free tier. Its repository lists "SSO, MFA, Passkeys, OIDC, SAML, SCIM" in one platform.

Who it's for

SaaS teams that give each customer company its own tenant and login rules.

Standout features

  • Unlimited organizations: the free plan lists "Unlimited Organizations 100 Daily Active Users".
  • Data residency: "Data Residency: EU, US, Switzerland, Australia".
  • Open source: the code ships under the AGPL-3.0 license.

Integrations

Apps connect over OIDC, SAML and SCIM; self-hosting runs on Docker or Kubernetes.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free is $0, Pro is US$ 100 /Month with 25,000 daily active users included; Enterprise is by contract.

Pros

  • Pro is a flat monthly price, not per seat.
  • Cloud and self-hosted run the same product.
  • Four data residency regions.

Cons

  • We found no prebuilt app catalog.
  • Free caps at 100 daily active users.
  • AGPL terms may need legal review.

Switch if

You build B2B software and need per-customer tenants with predictable monthly cost.

Stay with okta if

You mainly need staff SSO into SaaS apps from a catalog.

#6 Gc

Google Cloud Identity

Best for Google-first teams · 8.6 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Google Cloud Identity ↗

Google Cloud Identity manages users, devices and SSO for teams that don't need Gmail or other Workspace apps.

Why we picked it

It is the only option here bundling device management with SSO in a free tier. "Cloud Identity offers a free subscription option and a premium option with additional enterprise features for larger organizations".

Who it's for

Google Cloud or Chromebook-heavy teams that want identity and device control together.

Standout features

  • SAML SSO: both editions "Set up SSO using Google as an IdP to access third-party SAML" apps.
  • Context-Aware Access: Premium can "Control access based on user and device context (Context-Aware Access)".
  • 2-Step Verification: included in Free and Premium, with security keys.

Integrations

Third-party SAML apps, custom SAML apps, and automated user provisioning on Premium.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Cloud Identity Free costs nothing. The editions page we checked does not show the Premium per-user price.

Pros

  • Free edition includes SSO and 2-Step Verification.
  • Device management sits in the same console.
  • Premium lists a 99.9% SLA with 24x7 support.

Cons

  • Automated provisioning needs Premium.
  • No published Premium price on the editions page.
  • Cloud only.

Switch if

Your company already runs on Google Cloud and wants identity plus device control.

Stay with okta if

You need lifecycle workflows across many non-Google apps.

#7 Pi

Ping Identity

Best for large regulated enterprises · 7.8 /10 (editorial score) · Visit Ping Identity ↗

Ping Identity sells enterprise identity across shared cloud, single-tenant cloud and self-managed software.

Why we picked it

Ping has the widest deployment choice in this list. Buyers "Choose from fully-managed cloud, self-managed on-prem, hybrid, and FedRAMP-authorized environments".

Who it's for

Large enterprises and public agencies with strict hosting or compliance rules.

Standout features

  • Single-tenant cloud: "PingOne Advanced Identity Cloud is Ping’s single-tenant cloud platform".
  • Just-in-time privilege: an add-on can "Deliver just-in-time (JIT) access for sensitive operations".
  • Modular add-ons: orchestration, threat protection, identity verification and MFA are sold separately.

Integrations

The pricing page lists SSO and MFA among its services but names no app catalog or protocol list.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Ping publishes no prices; contact sales "for a quote tailored to your requirements".

Pros

  • FedRAMP-authorized environments are an option.
  • On-prem and hybrid deployment are documented.
  • You pay only for the add-ons you pick.

Cons

  • No published prices.
  • No free tier on the pricing page.
  • Add-on pricing makes costs hard to compare.

Switch if

You need on-prem, hybrid or FedRAMP hosting that Okta's cloud doesn't fit.

Stay with okta if

You want published per-user prices and a free trial.

FAQ

FAQs about Okta alternatives for

What is the cheapest Okta alternative?

OneLogin Basic at $3 per user a month is the cheapest per-user cloud plan we found. Microsoft Entra ID Free, Google Cloud Identity Free and self-hosted authentik cost nothing, with feature limits.

Is there an open-source Okta alternative?

Yes: authentik, Zitadel and FusionAuth Community all offer self-hosted versions. Zitadel ships under AGPL-3.0, and authentik says it will not move open-source features into its paid tier.

Does Okta have a minimum contract?

Yes, Okta Workforce Identity has a $1,500 annual contract minimum, and its suites bill annually. Okta Starter is $6 and Essentials $17 per user a month.

Which Okta alternative works best with Microsoft 365?

Microsoft Entra ID, because P1 ships inside Microsoft 365 E3 and P2 inside E5. Teams on those plans may already own the licenses they need.
