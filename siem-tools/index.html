Gr

Graylog

Best for open core with priced tiers

Graylog is an open core SIEM with a free self-managed edition and published prices for its Enterprise and Security tiers.

8.2/10 Visit Graylog

Why we picked it

Graylog is the only tool here with a free edition and a price on every paid tier. Graylog Open is "Always FREE", and the Security tier adds anomaly detection, automated workflows and AI incident reports.

Who it's for

Teams that want to start on free, self-managed log search and grow into a priced SIEM.

Standout features

  • Free open edition: Graylog Open is "Source-available under SSPL. No volume restrictions."
  • Anomaly detection: the Security tier lists "Machine-learning anomaly detection."
  • Guided response: Security includes "Guided response and automated workflows."
  • AI incident reports: "AI-generated incident reports with remediation recommendations" come with Security.
  • Two deployment paths: run it self-managed or on Graylog Cloud, also sold in the AWS Marketplace.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Graylog Open is free, Enterprise starts at $15,000/year and Security at $18,000/year, both from 10 GB/day.

Pros

  • Free edition has no volume restrictions.
  • Every paid tier shows a starting price.
  • SSO, LDAP roles and audit logs in Enterprise.

Cons

  • Paid tiers are priced per year only.
  • Anomaly detection and AI reports need the Security tier.
  • We found no SOC 2 report on its pricing page.

Blumira

Best for small teams, per-user pricing

Blumira is a cloud SIEM for small teams, priced per employee, with managed detections and one year of log retention.

7.8/10 Visit Blumira

Blumira takes full marks on pricing clarity: all three editions show a per-employee price on its pricing page. It pairs that with pre-tuned detections, compliance reports and one year of retention.

Small IT and security teams that want a SIEM without staffing a full SOC.

Standout features

  • Compliance reporting: Detect promises "compliance-ready reporting without alert overload."

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Detect is $12, Respond $16 and Automate $21 per employee per month; a 30-day trial covers Automate.

  • All three editions have a published price.
  • One year of log retention on every edition.
  • SAML is listed in additional functionality.

Cons

  • We found no free edition, only a 30-day trial.
  • Cloud only; we found no self-hosted option.
  • We found no UEBA or AI assistant on its pages.

FAQ

FAQs about SIEM tools

What is a SIEM tool?

A SIEM (security information and event management) tool collects logs from your systems, correlates them against detection rules and raises alerts. Most now add behavior analytics, threat intelligence and automated response playbooks.

Is there a free SIEM tool?

Yes. Wazuh is free and open source to self-host, and Graylog Open is free with no volume restrictions. Microsoft Sentinel also offers up to 5 MB/user/day of key logs free.

How much does a SIEM cost?

It varies widely. Blumira starts at $12 per employee per month, Wazuh Cloud at $571 a month and Graylog Enterprise at $15,000 a year. FortiSIEM and Splunk Enterprise Security are quote only.

Should I choose a cloud or self-hosted SIEM?

Choose cloud if you want no servers to run; Blumira, Datadog and Sentinel are cloud only. Choose self-hosted for data control or OT networks; Wazuh, Graylog, FortiSIEM and Splunk support it.

Which SIEM is best for a Microsoft environment?

Microsoft Sentinel. It ships connectors for Microsoft Entra ID and Azure Activity, builds playbooks on Azure Logic Apps and ingests key security logs free per user, per Microsoft's docs.
