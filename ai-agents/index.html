ChatGPT Work

Best overall

ChatGPT Work is OpenAI's agent for longer, multi-step tasks, and the only one here with full marks on four of our five criteria.

9.7/10 Visit ChatGPT Work

Why we picked it

Entry access scores full marks because the Free plan includes "Limited ChatGPT Work access on desktop". OpenAI's docs also cover every agent job, oversight control and admin element we check.

Who it's for

Teams on ChatGPT Business or Enterprise that want one agent for research, documents and app work.

Standout features

  • Plan mode: ChatGPT "asks questions, and creates a step-by-step plan", and you "approve the plan to start the work."
  • Approval check-ins: Work "checks in when judgment or approval matters", and you can "approve important actions".
  • Desktop mode: on the desktop app, Work "can also use local files, apps, and the browser".

Integrations

Integrations include Slack, Google Drive, SharePoint, Gmail, Outlook, Teams, Google Calendar and Salesforce, through ChatGPT Work plugins.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free and Go ($8/mo) run Work on desktop only; Business is $20/user/mo billed annually or $25 monthly; Enterprise is by sales.

Pros

  • Business includes SAML SSO and no training on business data by default.
  • Roles decide who uses Work; app permissions decide when it asks.
  • Admins can restrict Work Cloud's browser and shell network access.

Cons

  • Enterprise and Edu Work logs "don't establish a complete audit trail".
  • Local-file steps need the laptop "online and connected".
  • Work shares a usage pool with Codex; more use needs credits.

Claude Cowork

Best for approval controls

Claude Cowork is Anthropic's agent for everyday knowledge work, with approval modes an admin can restrict.

9.6/10 Visit Claude Cowork

Anthropic says it "uses the same agentic architecture that powers Claude Code, with no terminal required".

Why we picked it

Anthropic's docs cover all six agent jobs, including browser work where "Claude can open sites, read pages, click, type, and fill forms." Oversight and admin controls also score full marks, with SCIM, audit logs and retention controls on Enterprise.

Who it's for

Companies that want an admin, not each user, to decide how much the agent may do without asking.

Standout features

  • Manual mode: "Claude pauses and asks for approval for actions", and it needs explicit permission before permanently deleting files.
  • Admin mode lock: admins can turn off "Automatically approve," so it "doesn't appear in your members' mode selector."
  • Connector approvals: the Team and Enterprise setting that lets members always allow connector tools is off by default.

Integrations

Integrations include Slack, Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, Linear and Microsoft 365 through Claude connectors, plus Chrome via Claude in Chrome.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Pro is $17/mo billed annually or $20 monthly; Team Standard is $20/seat/mo annually or $25 monthly; Enterprise is $20/seat/mo annually (20-seat minimum) plus usage.

Pros

  • Team plans start at 2 seats, with no model training by default.
  • You can jump in to course-correct mid-task.
  • Anthropic lists ISO 27001, ISO/IEC 42001 and SOC 2.

Cons

  • Paid plans only: Claude's Free plan doesn't include Cowork.
  • Local sessions sit outside Anthropic's standard data retention policies.
  • Network egress rules don't cover web fetch, search or MCP tools.

Perplexity Computer

Best for per-site browser rules

Perplexity Computer is what Perplexity calls "an independent digital worker that completes tasks and workflows for you."

9.6/10 Visit Perplexity Computer

We score it with the Comet Assistant (Perplexity's browser agent) and Personal Computer (its Mac mode), which Perplexity sells alongside it.

Why we picked it

With those two, Perplexity covers all six agent jobs and ties Claude Cowork on every criterion. Perplexity Enterprise lists SSO, SCIM, audit logs and automatic file deletion, so admin controls score full marks.

Who it's for

Analysts, sales and product teams that want cited research turned into action in email, Slack or the browser.

Standout features

  • Per-site browser rules: an Enterprise admin sets each domain to Browser Control, Read Only or No Access.
  • Condition-based triggers: Computer can "monitor your email, calendar, flight status, and files with condition-based triggers".
  • Personal Computer: on a Mac it works across local files and apps, and "Actions are auditable and reversible."

Integrations

Integrations include Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Notion, Linear and Salesforce through Computer's connectors.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Pro is $20/mo or $200/yr with no monthly Computer credits; Max is $200/mo with 10,000; Enterprise Pro is $40/seat/mo.

Pros

  • Every tier, including Enterprise Max, has a published price.
  • Computer runs in "a secure cloud sandbox" by default.
  • The Comet Assistant asks before acting unless admins enable "Always Allow."

Cons

  • Computer doesn't undo changes it already made in connected apps.
  • Free plan has no browser-agent queries; Computer needs a paid plan.
  • Per-site rules are Enterprise-only, and Personal Computer rolled out to Max first.

Manus

Best standalone agent

Manus is a standalone agent that "browses the web in an isolated browser in the cloud", not a chat-assistant feature.

9.3/10 Visit Manus

Its desktop app, Manus Studio, lets it "work with the files, terminal, and apps on your computer." Meta acquired Manus on December 29, 2025, and Manus says it "has formally resumed independent operations" as of September 1, 2026.

Why we picked it

With ChatGPT Work, it is one of two agents here with full marks on entry access: free to use and $25 or less a month to upgrade. Its docs also cover every agent job and oversight control.

Who it's for

Small teams and individuals who want one agent for research, slides, websites and app tasks.

Standout features

  • Three permission modes: from "Ask for approval," which "Always asks for approval before local actions", to "Full access."
  • Automations: tasks run on a schedule or app event, and each run shows a status and summary.
  • Takeover: "the user can take control of Manus's computer, operating the browser or VS Code".

Integrations

Integrations include Gmail, Google Calendar, Outlook, Slack, Notion, HubSpot, Asana and Canva, from more than 400 connectors.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Free gives 300 credits a day, Pro starts at $20/mo and Team at $20/seat/mo with 4,000 pooled credits per seat.

Pros

  • Team adds an admin dashboard and audit logs.
  • Manus states: "We never allow model training on your data."
  • Security page lists SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001 and ISO 27701.

Cons

  • Meta separation required some users to back up and restore data.
  • SSO is a $150 add-on below 30 seats.
  • We found no SCIM or data retention setting in Manus's pages.

Microsoft Copilot Cowork

Best for Microsoft 365 shops

Copilot Cowork is the agent inside Microsoft Copilot for work accounts, and it "inherits your permissions."

8.3/10 Visit Microsoft Copilot Cowork

Microsoft says it "can send emails, schedule meetings, create documents (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF), post in Teams, manage your calendar," and more.

Why we picked it

Microsoft documents all five oversight controls, including a "temporary, isolated environment inside the Microsoft 365 service boundary". Admin and data controls score 9, with SCIM the only element we didn't find.

Who it's for

Companies on Microsoft 365 that want an agent working under their existing permissions, audit and Purview policies.

Standout features

  • Risk-rated approvals: sensitive actions get "an approval prompt", risk-rated when medium or high; repeats can be skipped per recipient.
  • Pause and cancel: you can pause, resume or cancel a running task.
  • Local Edge tasks: Cowork does web tasks in Microsoft Edge on your device, and admins can turn this off.

Integrations

Integrations include OneDrive, SharePoint, OneNote and Edge, plus connectors from Microsoft 365 App Store plugins.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, the Microsoft 365 Copilot Business add-on is $21/user/mo billed yearly ($25.20 monthly); eligible existing Business customers buying by December 31 pay $18 the first year.

Pros

  • Prompts and responses "aren't used to train foundation models".
  • By default, automated tasks ask before sending, posting or changing shared systems.
  • Admins grant access only through a spending policy.

Cons

  • Coding agent Copilot Code is a Frontier preview, so it doesn't count.
  • "Cowork can't access or edit files stored locally on your device."
  • Cowork usage is billed in Copilot Credits on top of the license.

Best for Notion workspaces

Notion Custom Agents are agents that start on a schedule or when something happens in Notion or Slack.

8.3/10 Visit Notion Custom Agents

Notion says "Custom Agents live inside Notion and run on your instructions." Notion Agent handles on-demand, multi-step requests on the same Business plan.

Why we picked it

Admin and data controls score full marks, from SAML SSO on Business to custom data retention on Enterprise. Counting Notion Agent, they cover four of our six agent jobs.

Who it's for

Teams whose docs and projects live in Notion and who want recurring work done in the background.

Standout features

  • Activity tab: each Custom Agent logs every run, with its trigger, actions and errors, and version history restores earlier setups.
  • Explicit access: "Agents act only on the pages, databases, and external apps you explicitly grant access to."
  • Write confirmation: "Write actions (like sending) will ask you to confirm before Notion Agent takes action."

Integrations

Integrations include Slack, Gmail and, through AI connectors, Google Drive, Jira, Microsoft Teams, OneDrive and SharePoint.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026, Business is $20/member/mo; Custom Agents are free to try, then $10 per 1,000 monthly credits; Free and Plus get a trial.

Pros

  • Notion AI won't train on your data unless you opt in.
  • Notion's security page lists SOC 2 and ISO 27001.
  • Agents can post and reply in Slack threads.

Cons

  • We found no browser actions on websites or access to your computer.
  • We found no documented way to watch or stop a live run.
  • SCIM, audit log and model-provider zero retention are Enterprise-only.

Zapier Agents

Best for agents across many apps

Zapier Agents are agents that work on command or when a trigger fires, and Zapier says they can "perform tasks across 9,000+ apps."

7.8/10 Visit Zapier Agents

Zapier is moving Agents to AI by Zapier, an agent step in Zaps billed in tasks, and "has not set a date to turn off Agents yet."

Why we picked it

Admin and data controls score full marks, from SAML SSO and a 6-month audit log on Team to SCIM and custom retention on Enterprise. It ranks seventh because Zapier documents only two of our six agent jobs.

Who it's for

Teams that already automate with Zapier and want an agent for steps that need judgment.

Standout features

  • Per-run cap: Free allows 10 activities per run and paid plans 40, a safeguard for the monthly quota.
  • Approval steps: an instruction like "ask for my confirmation through [messaging app]" makes the agent wait for you to confirm.
  • Enterprise app access: admins can limit which apps members use in Zaps and Agents.

Integrations

Agents use apps connected through Zapier; Zapier's agent templates name Slack, Google Docs and Zendesk.

Pricing

As of Nov 2026: Free with 400 activities a month; Agents Pro is $33.33/mo billed annually ($400 a year) with 1,500; Enterprise is by sales.

Pros

  • Agent activities don't use your Zap task allowance.
  • Zapier doesn't train on workflow data, on any plan.
  • Zapier's security page lists SOC 2 (Type II) and SOC 3.

Cons

  • Zapier calls Agents "an early beta" and doesn't guarantee support replies.
  • Pricing FAQ and platform page disagree on Agents' action restrictions; confirm first.
  • At the activity limit, "Your agents will no longer run."

FAQ

FAQs about AI agents

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that takes a goal and works through the steps itself, using a browser, apps, code or files, instead of only replying in chat. For answers and drafts rather than actions, the assistants on our AI tools list fit better.

What is the best free AI agent?

ChatGPT Work is the top-rated free option, then Manus; our free AI tools list goes wider. Perplexity's Free plan lists the Comet Assistant, but its plan guide gives Free no browser-agent queries, and Computer needs a paid plan beyond one-time free turns on some accounts.

Is it safe to let an AI agent use my email and company apps?

Only with limits: Microsoft says Cowork "inherits your permissions", so it can act on anything you can access unless an approval step stops it. Start in an approval-required mode, connect only the apps a task needs, and check the activity record.

Do AI agents replace automation tools like Zapier or n8n?

Not for fixed steps: a workflow runs the steps you set, while an agent picks its own, which suits varied inputs. You can combine them: AI by Zapier puts an agent step in a Zap, and n8n's AI Agent node can pause for approval.

ChatGPT Work or Claude Cowork?

Pick ChatGPT Work for a free start, since Cowork needs a paid plan; pick Claude Cowork if admins must lock approval modes. Both team plans cost $20 per seat a month billed annually.
